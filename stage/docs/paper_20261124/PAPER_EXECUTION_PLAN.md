## Fixed paper question

Under a comparable deployment-memory budget, what does finite executed-command preservation add over ordinary interpolation and replay, and what retention/transfer/cost tradeoff remains outside protected histories?

Provisional working title: Finite Policy Retention in Recurrent Navigation: Compact Interpolation, Replay and Closed-Loop Limits.

This is a testable paper question, not a claim of achieved superiority or top-journal readiness. Frozen expansion, radial interpolation and imitation-learning distribution shift have established precedents. The scope must be assessed against [Progressive Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04671), [DAgger](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) and the more detailed primary reviews already in the repo. These papers do not certify the Stage implementation or supply its novelty.

## Minimum next experimental block, subject to preregistration

Keep T703 compact protected policy and B0 ordinary replay as named references. Before a new native run, check whether removing old-history distances from T703's radius rule would actually change the support function. If it is identical, record a vacuous ablation and spend no native budget repeating it.

If non-vacuous, construct one compact plain-support comparator: same frozenT694,184-to8 projection,1737centers,sourcecoefficients,radius dtype,source data and deployment arithmetic; remove only the old-history separation term from the predetermined radius rule. Independently check source fitting and finite arithmetic. Old-history equality is an outcome, not an admission condition for this plain comparator. Do not tune radius multipliers after native results. This isolates whether the compact protection construction provides value rather than merely comparing large interpolation with small MLP.

This comparator alone does not establish state-of-the-art performance. A paper also needs a finite, validation-selected ordinary replay retention tradeoff under comparable memory and declared training work. Existing B0 is a credible reference, not a certified optimum. Fix a small tuning allowance, family-based development split and selection rule before training; match method-development/tuning budgets fairly. Report all points and actual FLOPs/time, including failures. No zero-forgetting screen may censor completed candidates from evaluation.
