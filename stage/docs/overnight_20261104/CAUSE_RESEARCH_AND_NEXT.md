### Exact latent/float32/recurrent likelihood contract

Let `m_t(theta)` be the current actor's unclipped location reconstructed by whole-sequence causal BPTT on the **recorded observed states and executed past actions**. Record raw latent `u_t` and execute `a_t=float32(clip(u_t,-1,1))`. For stationary AR1 residuals:

```
u_0 ~ Normal(m_0, sigma^2)
q_t(theta) = m_t(theta) + rho * (u_(t-1) - m_(t-1)(theta))
u_t | u_<t, observed_history ~ Normal(q_t(theta), sigma^2 * (1-rho^2)), t>0
```

At sigma .20/rho exp(-.2), innovation SD is about **.11484**, whereas the stationary residual SD remains .20. Start with stationary initial residual, or explicitly document a zero-start transient; do not call zero-start stationary. In the score graph, saved `u_(t-1)` is fixed sampled data; **both current `m_t` and current `m_(t-1)` carry gradients**, so the conditional location derivative includes `d m_t - rho*d m_(t-1)`. Do not detach the previous location, reuse old hidden states, substitute clipped actions for raw past latents, or hold an old normalized residual fixed while parameters move. Reconstruct the whole GRU history with current parameters, preserving episode reset boundaries and no future inputs. With rho0 this reduces exactly to ordinary raw Gaussian log-density; preflight should verify this and actual spawn action/raw/logp agreement, score finite differences and clone isolation without native stepping.

**Use the full raw latent Gaussian score at every step in both arms**, including tails. It is a valid augmented latent trajectory likelihood for the executed clipped policy objective; it can have greater variance than a valid collapsed estimator. The AR's raw past tail magnitude influences future residuals, so blindly replacing a tail's score by independent CAPG mass discards information that can affect future return. A derivation integrating the entire affected latent future would be needed for a collapsed alternative. Do not cite CAPG's ordinary action-bound theorem as that missing derivation. This warning is a derivation for this project's proposed latent implementation, informed by CAPG's conditions and the AR conditional-policy construction.

For T351's iid collapsed implementation, the +1 float32 atom starts at the midpoint `EDGE=(1+nextafter_float32(1,0))/2 = .9999999701976776` (and its negative). Atom likelihood is tail mass at those thresholds; the interior currently records **raw continuous latent density**, not exact discrete float32 bin mass. Match claims to the chosen measure. Float32 execution is many-to-one, but full raw likelihood avoids pretending a rounded interior action is a density sample from an executed continuous distribution. Never mix a raw numerator with an atom-mass denominator, omit conditional innovation normalization, or compare AR conditional ratios against iid old logp. Fresh arm data must store its own exact behavior logp; any iid-vs-AR action tape reused for offline diagnostics is not on-policy data for the other arm.

PPO per-step clipped ratios are a surrogate under policy change, not a hard KL bound or an unbiased full-trajectory IS correction. Keeping recurrent histories correct does not alter that limitation. Treat synthetic score checks as numerical validation, not physical success.
