# Retention: primary sources and one bounded next mechanism

2026-11-20. Research note only: no simulator, optimizer, training, or scientific run was executed for this review. Only this document was written. Stage evidence only; no Phase research was inspected. This is a finite empirical proposal, not a safety certificate, universal nonforgetting result, or novelty claim.

## Recommendation

After the already authorized actual-failure full-history collection, test **one causal external-history response-retention term**, beside the identical nominal-retention PPO baseline. First run a frozen diagnostic; enter learning only if it establishes that the proposed response quantity is numerically correct, nontrivial, and relevant to the observed small-departure histories. Do not add a cost critic, new dynamics model, policy cascade, CPO projection, new network capacity, recovery teacher, or deployment switch to this comparison.

The scientific question is narrow: does retaining the controller's local response to *observed causal-history departures* preserve more old original-start successes at the same new-task learning and interaction budget than retaining its nominal outputs alone? A response penalty is established prior art; its usefulness for this particular recurrent vehicle remains untested.

## Five primary references and their precise gaps

1. **Pfrommer et al., TaSIL, NeurIPS 2022; arXiv v2 (2023), §§2–4 and Appendix C.** It augments action imitation with policy Taylor-term matching, relating expert-trajectory fitting to learner-trajectory behavior under incremental input-to-state stability and regularity assumptions. Stage has not established these assumptions, a suitable augmented recurrent state, or a trajectory margin to collision/goal boundaries. A directional derivative in externally supplied observations is weaker than its state-feedback premise. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.14812v2). The author's [BC entry](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unstable-zeros/TaSIL/main/imitation_learning/bc.py) builds expert data; [DAgger entry](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unstable-zeros/TaSIL/main/imitation_learning/dagger.py) aggregates new mixed-policy data. These entrypoints were read, not executed or fully audited; no claim of an exact author-code reproduction.

2. **Achiam et al., CPO, ICML 2017, §§3–6, Algorithm 1.** It separates expected reward from expected auxiliary-cost constraints and computes a local constrained trust-region update. Practical estimates, conjugate-gradient solves, and line search approximate the ideal problem; the paper explicitly discusses infeasible iterates and recovery. Its expected-cost objective is not per-ID deterministic retention. A zero failure-cost expectation with exact full-support evaluation would be much stronger than a finite noisy estimate; the latter cannot certify 384 IDs. Sparse successful deterministic histories also provide little constraint-gradient information. Recurrent application needs complete history/time state and correct behavior densities. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a/achiam17a.pdf).

3. **Yang et al., PCPO, ICLR 2020; arXiv v1, §§2–4.** It performs a reward step then projects against a cost constraint. This addresses recovering feasible updates without merely tuning a fixed penalty weight. Practical updates linearize cost and approximate policy distance quadratically; the analysis requires conditions on the policy metric, smoothness and constraint geometry. It does not turn a finite set of zero-loss output anchors into a true closed-loop feasible region. Per-task constraints and an active set are possible engineering extensions, but each needs valid gradients and cost evidence; selecting fewer constraints supplies no automatic coverage guarantee. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.03152v1). OpenReview PDF encountered browser verification; the arXiv full text was used instead.

4. **Ross, Gordon and Bagnell, DAgger, AISTATS 2011, §3, Algorithm 3.1.** Its gap is training occupancy versus learner occupancy: collect the learner's visited inputs, query expert actions there, aggregate, and retrain. No-regret/reduction assumptions and expert quality matter. T501's nominal GOAL does not establish corrective competence on another policy's history; T659R contains counterexamples. For a GRU, the expert must process the actual external prefix to derive its own carry. Relabeling nominal actions by timestamp or treating every reference query as a correct recovery label is invalid. Student-occupancy collection is useful evidence, but without competent correction it is not a demonstrated DAgger fix. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a/ross11a.pdf).

5. **Kaplanis et al., Policy Consolidation, ICML 2019, §§3 and 4.3.1.** A cascade distills policies across timescales without task-boundary labels. This addresses a moving reference and continual experience distributions. It adds training networks and cost; it does not require task-ID heads in deployment. The authors explicitly distinguish conditional one-step KL from trajectory-distribution preservation, observe hidden policies need not remain coherent, and discuss high-variance trajectory importance ratios. Therefore a cascade would not by itself address Stage's measured tiny-output/large-trajectory gap or establish strict old-ID retention. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/kaplanis19a/kaplanis19a.pdf).

These sources justify discriminating three gaps: **nominal response**, **visited-history coverage**, and **actual outcome constraints**. They do not justify combining all three algorithms into one weekend method.

## Frozen diagnostic: define the quantity before fitting it

Use the four actual lost-task pairs named by the supervisor: T501 reference versus low-binary candidate on old20/21, and versus collision candidate on old23/hold6_06. They are selected diagnostics, not held-out or random tasks. The supervisor has fixed this collection at eight full trajectories plus eight exact replays, at most 373,344 physical steps and 22 commands, with zero updates. Retain both complete external histories and exact replays, including failure endpoints, all actions, ownership, stochastic flags, time, goal, scene, native state and source/policy hashes. Compact outcomes without observation histories cannot support this analysis. Do not restart a failed trial to manufacture a successful label.

For matched physical decision times before either trajectory terminates, define `H_r,t = (o_r,0,...,o_r,t)` and `H_c,t` from the actual 120-input packets. Define `D_t = H_c,t - H_r,t` after the existing fixed normalization. Every packet contains its actual applied-action/history fields; preserve them together. Do not independently noise 120 columns, edit the goal, insert proposed-but-unapplied actions, inject reference hidden state, or call an interpolated packet sequence physically reachable.

A small fixed construction is sufficient: on each of four pairs, choose eight evenly spaced decision indices from reset through the first observed 1 cm position separation, inclusive (deduplicate if needed). Freeze the chosen indices before seeing derivative results. This gives at most 32 prefix directions. Each `D_t` includes the **whole prefix**; it is not only the last observation or a freely chosen hidden perturbation. The 1 cm marker defines a diagnostic window and supplies no safety margin.

For actual deployed command `a_theta(H)=clip(location_theta(H),-1,1)`, measure

`R_theta,t = d/d alpha a_theta(H_r,t + alpha D_t) at alpha=0`.

Also retain the finite observed-history secant `S_theta,t = a_theta(H_c,t)-a_theta(H_r,t)`. A JVP is an infinitesimal derivative of the network on a specified external sequence; a secant is a finite difference. Neither is a physical closed-loop derivative: intermediate histories may violate sensor/dynamics consistency, and the simulator is not differentiated. Normalization and packet ownership must match runtime. Compute each policy's carry by replay from reset; no nominal carry may be spliced after divergence.

The safe first implementation route is full `q.sequence` differentiation with respect to the input prefix. A later exact cache may include the frozen core's feature JVP and adapter mixed derivatives, but only after comparison with full-sequence gradients. Detaching nominal `f/logits` drops required input paths. Because the backbone is fixed, its input derivative is shared, but adapter response and parameter gradients are not zero. Include past applied-action coordinates through the complete input direction; this still does not differentiate the environment or future control feedback. Report executed-command derivatives and clipping status, with preclip derivatives only as diagnostic supplements.

**Predeclared gate:** numerical directional differences must match autograd (absolute tolerance 1e-8 plus relative 1e-4 in float64, away from clip kinks); saturated/kink cases remain explicit, not silently discarded. Require at least 16 nonzero, differentiable prefix responses across at least three pairs, and relative JVP-to-secant residual below 25% on at least three pairs within this small-departure window. These thresholds are engineering falsification criteria, not paper-derived guarantees. Report every residual, including failures. If the gate fails, stop this derivative branch; do not add derivative order/noise magnitude/weight grids to rescue it. If it passes, it only supports trying the comparison below.

## Finite matched learning comparison

Start both arms from the **same frozen checkpoint, sigma and objective** after the current full407 evidence is sealed. Use the currently chosen low-noise collision-cost PPO configuration identically in both arms; source/initial policy hashes must agree. Reuse one existing 64-trajectory first block only if its policy hash, sigma, objective semantics and full-causal-density contract exactly match this starting policy. Otherwise collect exactly one shared fresh block. Data collected under a different candidate is not on-policy for this comparison.

- Baseline: unchanged unique-reference nominal retention plus PPO.
- Treatment: the same objective plus `lambda * mean ||R_theta,t - R_ref,t||^2`, using the frozen 32 directions/targets above. Retain nominal action loss in both arms. Normalize both command differences and directional responses by the existing 0.2 command scale; set lambda equal to the already fixed nominal replay coefficient. This is one declared empirical choice, not an optimal weight. Do not estimate lambda from the response-loss gradient at initialization: it can be exactly zero when the reference and learner agree. Do not select it by native outcomes. No hidden input, planner, router, runtime teacher or task-ID head is added.

Initial ceiling: one candidate per arm, four live Actor steps per arm, matched shadow and independent reconstruction; same four update opportunities, data, optimizer initialization, nominal weights, clipping and rejection rules. This is a mechanism pilot, not a repeated-seed result. Set a separate wall/physical/storage ceiling before registration, deducting all diagnostics and audits. Full-sequence response work costs more than nominal work: report wall time, peak memory and extra backward/JVP calls; give the baseline the same native budget, not falsely claim equal FLOPs. No second round or extra seed is implied by unused time.

Freeze each resulting candidate. Measure the identical 36 screen IDs and 20 exact controls; only a candidate preserving required old screen IDs and adding a genuine original-start success receives the remaining 371 tests. Thus the worst evaluation ceiling is `2*(36+20+371)=854` episode commands, plus the shared 64+64 exact training-history commands if newly collected, and explicitly budgeted diagnostics. Native-step limits must be summed from actual task horizons, not inferred from average run time. Keep per-ID gained/lost sets and termination/distance, not only totals. If gates prevent full407, report it as unmeasured; do not call missing entries failures or successes.

**Stop and decision:** any causal/density/ownership/numerical or independent-reconstruction failure stops the branch with raw evidence intact. Scientific success requires zero loss of the original384 plus at least one original-start gain and independent full407 verification of that same checkpoint. Compare treatment versus matched baseline on both gains and losses. Smaller derivative loss, later collision, or rejection of every update is not progress. If treatment cannot outperform baseline in retained improvement within this one fixed pilot, record the mechanism as unsupported here and do not sweep lambda/order/seeds under the same protocol. Existing low/collision candidates and every old/new failure remain unchanged as counterexamples. Neighboring starts/layouts and real-vehicle validity require separate later evidence.
