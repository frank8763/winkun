# T695 frozen initial-state neighborhood: bounded primary-source review

2026-11-22, Stage only. **One predefined, physically valid, paired initial-position benchmark of frozen T694 against frozen T501 is defensible. Its claim is finite local initial-state robustness around six exposed development starts. It cannot distinguish interpolation from broadly transferable navigation.** This review admits no training, extra centers, coefficient changes, randomized/adversarial search, amplitude grid, or automatic follow-up campaign. It is research advice, not a registered protocol or launch approval.

## Starting evidence and the question

[T694 findings](t694/FINDINGS.md) report one retained candidate with 390/407 safe successes, every original384 success retained, all six repair sources successful, and the historical union387 passed. Its frozen T501 body has64772 parameters; the extension stores1405 fixed184-dimensional centers/radii and2810 supervised coefficients. The six original starts are field0_1, field0_2, field1_1, field2_1, field5_1 and field7_1. They were development/fit sources, not blind test tasks. The relevant next question is whether this already frozen repair also succeeds at specific nearby physical starts and how frozen T501 behaves on exactly those starts. These local facts are taken from the report, not independently recomputed here.

The [preceding exact-command review](EXACT_COMMAND_RETENTION_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md) proves only a conditional finite-trajectory induction: identical complete initial state, causal reset/history and executed commands reproduce the same deterministic trajectory. It does not extend equality to perturbed histories. In particular, separation from77589 old feature points and an identity interpolation matrix on1405 source features do not give a robustness radius in physical meters. A changed pose changes observations; differing actions subsequently change each policy's observations and its own recurrent carry. That is a project-specific inference from the described construction, not a new stability theorem.

## Three primary sources actually read

1. **Zhang, Vinyals, Munos and Bengio, _A Study on Overfitting in Deep Reinforcement Learning_, arXiv1804.06893v2 (20 April2018).** Read §3 opening/§3.1 evaluation setup, §4 random-start and stochastic evaluation results, Fig.3 caption, and §5 discussion. Their maze study separates training and test initial states. It also demonstrates agents that retain high scores under random starts or sticky actions on training mazes while performing poorly on unseen mazes. Therefore successful perturbation tests can coexist with memorization. Their discrete mazes and A3C experiments do not establish a failure probability or suitable metric amplitude for Stage. [Original paper, pp.3,6–8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.06893).

2. **Cobbe et al., _Quantifying Generalization in Reinforcement Learning_, ICML2019.** Read §§3.1–3.2 and the architecture-comparison passage in §4. CoinRun separates train/test levels, evaluates without parameter fine-tuning, and reports train/test behavior. Its protocol reinforces exposure accounting and frozen evaluation. Its procedurally generated level distribution is different from a deterministic stencil around six known Stage starts; those starts cannot inherit CoinRun's distributional interpretation or experimental sample size. [Published original paper, pp.2–4](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/cobbe19a/cobbe19a.pdf).

3. **MuJoCo official documentation, version3.2.1, _Computation_.** Read “The State,” “Consistency in mjData,” and “Reproducibility.” The documentation distinguishes integrated state and user inputs, warns that derived quantities need explicit recomputation after state changes, and requires complete integration state including warmstarts for bitwise restoration. Exact reproduction is confined to the same simulator version/architecture; tiny numerical differences can grow during integration. This is an official simulator contract, not evidence that Stage's reset implementation already satisfies it. [State](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.2.1/computation/index.html#the-state), [consistency and replay](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.2.1/computation/index.html#consistency-in-mjdata).

The following benchmark recommendations are engineering deductions for this fixed comparison. None of these sources prescribes a Stage amplitude, certifies safety, or requires a broad new training-seed campaign for this descriptive test.

## A sufficient small benchmark

Prefer **four signed translation directions per anchor**, expressed in one declared world-coordinate frame:

`(+delta,0,0), (-delta,0,0), (0,+delta,0), (0,-delta,0)` for `(x,y,yaw)`.

This is24 distinct perturbed starts across the six anchors and48 primary policy episodes. Six-axis signed testing would instead add `(0,0,+alpha)` and `(0,0,-alpha)`, giving36 starts and72 primary episodes. These are alternatives to choose before results, not sequential opportunities to expand a favorable result. Translation-only is sufficient for the stated small initial-position question; yaw is unnecessary unless orientation robustness is explicitly part of the question. No diagonals, Cartesian product, random draws, trajectory disturbances or adversarial best-of selection are needed.

Choose and lock **one positive delta in meters** before either policy is evaluated. The papers do not justify a particular number; a value such as0.02m would be an explicit engineering choice, not a literature-derived threshold. Its admissibility must come from the actual geometry/reset contract and useful resolution above numerical noise, independent of policy success. If yaw is selected, lock one alpha in radians/degrees separately: meters and angles do not share one numerical amplitude. Record actual represented offsets so quantization cannot silently turn an intended perturbation into the nominal start. This note selects no final numeric amplitude.

Construct every start from the same canonical anchor with unchanged obstacles, fixed world goal, velocity/actuator initialization, physical time/decision schedule and terminal rules. Translating both vehicle and goal would test a different question. Change only the intended pose coordinates using the actual vehicle's valid pose representation; initialize and recompute dependent sensor/kinematic state through the canonical reset path. Do not perturb120 observation columns independently or splice old features into a new physical start. Geometry admissibility includes finite valid state, vehicle footprint/obstacle and applicable boundary constraints, and a nonterminal start under the existing goal rule. These checks do not prove that a collision-free path to the goal exists.

All specified starts must pass the policy-independent validity gate before science. An invalid case must remain explicit; do not silently drop it, shrink only that offset, resample a seed or substitute an easier neighbor. If the chosen family cannot be admitted, report a geometry-admission failure and resolve it before evaluation, without looking at policy outcomes. This prevents a moving denominator and success-conditioned neighborhood choice.

## What must be matched and reported

- Freeze both complete artifacts and hashes, including T694 centers/radii/coefficients. Pair by exact physical initial-state and task hashes, not a shared seed label alone. Use the same simulator/build/device, action clipping/float32 conversion, control holds, observations, stop rules and applicable boundaries. Give each policy its own canonical reset and causal recurrent history; after the first different action, do not force equal trajectories or copy hidden state between policies.
- Report nominal-anchor results separately from the24 or36 perturbed starts. Nominal source reproduction is already known; it must not inflate the neighborhood denominator. Existing exact nominal evidence can be referenced with identity checks rather than automatically rerun. Independent replay, if budgeted by the parent, is an audit count alongside primary episodes.
- Preserve one row per anchor/direction for both policies: safe GOAL, collision, timeout, applicable boundary failure, or engineering error/missing. Record arrival time for successes and remaining distance for failures without substituting distance or return for safe GOAL. Give per-anchor counts and the four paired outcomes: both safe, T694-only safe, T501-only safe, neither safe. The signed difference in safe counts is descriptive; show both gains and losses.
- Keep exposure honest. The anchors were used for development, and T694 fitted full histories from them. A newly enumerated offset is not automatically historically unseen: check available consumption records, and label unknown prior exposure as unknown. Even if its exact reset was never consumed, it remains near an exposed task in the same scene. Do not use these outcomes to tune supports, select a checkpoint, add centers or adjust amplitude and continue calling the same panel untouched.

This is **matched evaluation of two artifacts**, not same-data/same-training-budget algorithm comparison: T694 has supervised source exposure and extra representation beyond T501. A favorable paired result cannot attribute improvement to an original RL method, fixed-capacity continual learning or a particular operator independent of capacity/data.

## Deterministic replay and interpretation limits

An exact rerun of the same state and frozen policy checks implementation/serialization consistency under the documented runtime conditions. It does not add an independent initial-state sample. The24 translation cases are also a structured finite set clustered under six exposed anchors, not24 IID draws from an operational distribution. Do not attach binomial confidence intervals, a universal failure probability or statistical significance to exact reruns or this enumerated panel.

If T694 succeeds on all prescribed offsets, the justified statement is: **“Frozen T694 safely reached the fixed goals from all24 predefined valid ±x/±y starts at delta in this simulator; frozen T501 reached them from N/24.”** This establishes behavior at those points. It does not certify the interior of a ball, intermediate amplitudes, diagonal combinations, yaw (if omitted), other anchors/maps, observation noise or disturbances during motion. Passing remains compatible with compact-support interpolation and memorization, as the first primary source cautions. A loss at an offset is a concrete local failure, not proof that the whole scene is unreachable or that every interpolation method fails.

T694's already verified390 nominal successes remain the protection reference for subsequent learning. This frozen six-anchor panel does not replace full407 evidence or authorize modifying the model. Both successful and failed pairs are useful final evidence; no broad statistical seed campaign or extra diagnostic ladder is necessary to complete this bounded question.
