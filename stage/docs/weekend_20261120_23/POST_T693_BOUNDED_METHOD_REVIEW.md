## 1. Tail-risk outcome learning instead of the mean terminal objective

**Potential difference:** a static coherent-risk/CVaR objective on actual episode outcomes would change the population objective, rather than merely changing a baseline (T690), projecting the same sampled objective (T674), or penalizing the worst supervised action errors. This is conceptually distinct from the **reference-loss** tail aggregation already present in [T660 plan](../overnight_20261119/PLAN.md): mean plus worst10% tasks/worst20% time segments. It must not be advertised as Stage's first tail-sensitive method.

[Tamar et al., NeurIPS2015, published paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/024d7f84fff11dd7e8d9c510137a2381-Paper.pdf), §§2,4–4.3, derive risk-envelope policy gradients, including CVaR and discrete outcomes. Static risk uses trajectory sampling; the dynamic variant requires value approximation. The paper supplies no guarantee that improving stochastic tail risk preserves every deterministic initial condition. Its regularity/risk-envelope assumptions and asymptotic estimator properties are not finite Stage retention guarantees.

**Local obstruction and explicit inference:** terminal outcomes here have atoms; a continuous-return tail formula cannot be applied without their mass/tie treatment. For the illustrative binary collision cost C∈{0,1}, collision probability p and upper-tail mass α, the CVaR definition gives `CVaR_alpha(C)=min(p/alpha,1)`. Thus when p>α the true objective is locally saturated, not an automatically stronger collision gradient. This identity is an elementary derivation for that binary example, **not** a measured distribution for every Stage task. With safeGOAL−collision's additional timeout atom, the exact cases differ but atoms and saturation remain relevant. Choosing a tail mass/mixture after inspecting sparse failures would introduce a new unsupported tuning choice, while task pooling still permits one old ID to compensate another. A per-task risk formulation still concerns noisy execution, not deterministic deployment.

[T672 support](t672/SUPPORT_REVIEW.json) and [T673](t673/FINDINGS.md), as summarized in current RESEARCH_STATUS, already show many outcomes with no within-task contrast, few field contributors and sensitive estimates. Outcome-tail reweighting does not create missing outcome distinctions. The [actual T660R report](../overnight_20261119/recovery/FINAL_REPORT.json) and [T660S report](../overnight_20261119/resume/FINAL_REPORT.json) preserve completed, independently audited mean/tail learning children with no retained best model; their later root failures do not erase these child results. Those tail penalties are not an exact CVaR-outcome trial, so they do not disprove it.

**Reject now:** no principled fixed risk specification, supported informative gradient and plausible complete same-budget retention comparison were established together. No risk-level/weight/seed search or preliminary risk-diagnostic ladder is proposed.

## 2. Optimize a deterministic target policy with an action-value derivative

**Potential difference:** changing the continuation/occupancy target from pi_sigma to pi_0 addresses the objective discrepancy more directly than another stochastic estimator. It is not the unlaunched T693 estimator, whose future continuation remained stochastic. Merely using deterministic suffixes with the Gaussian MVD formula would still differentiate a smoothed current-action objective, and would not by itself produce the deterministic whole-policy gradient.

[Silver et al., ICML2014, published paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/silver14.pdf), §3.2 Theorem1 and §4, express the deterministic gradient using the action derivative of Q under the target policy and its occupancy; existence requires the stated regularity conditions. Their actor-critic uses differentiable Q approximations/compatibility conditions. The experiments include capped actions, but do not validate the theorem's derivative or critic approximation for Stage's quantized commands, contact/goal events and recurrent sensor histories.

**Local obstruction:** no validated deterministic-target action-value derivative is available. [T622](../day_20261117/t622/FINDINGS.md) used mixed continuation labels and failed to establish current-action usefulness. [T625R](../day_20261117/t625r/FINDINGS.md) retained its failed producer and recovery separately; the heldout branch gap was only 2.55e-6 and calibration failed. T692 removed mixed behavior but failed heldout action-input gates before native testing; this does not validate Q for pi_0. [T659R](../day_20261119/t659r/FINDINGS.md) additionally warns that a single changed action followed by the old policy can both admit and reject the wrong complete-policy candidates. None proves that all critics fail.

Replacing Q with a few finite native branch slopes would need a justified derivative target and informative finite scale; differentiating terminal event outcomes is not automatically justified by differentiability of the neural Actor. Replacing it with whole-episode parameter smoothing returns toward [T679](t679/FINDINGS.md)/[T680](t680/FINDINGS.md): four directions in11906 dimensions failed the fixed stability/support gates, and no center update occurred. This is not an invitation to increase directions or change amplitude.

**Reject now:** the genuinely different deterministic objective has no locally validated credit operator. Importing an unvalidated Q or renaming a finite branch slope would bypass the missing evidence. No new critic/branch queue or sigma reduction is selected.

## 3. Preserve finite feedback response rather than nominal actions

**Potential difference:** matching the local policy's response to physical/state-history deviations aims at feedback amplification, rather than average nominal command error or outcome-score geometry. Higher-order response matching would differ from first-order matching, but is already part of the same TaSIL-inspired family considered in this campaign; changing derivative order alone is not established as a new justified mechanism.

[Pfrommer et al., TaSIL, NeurIPS2022, published paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/7f10c3d66c3b7863a9cda255dcac5bb7-Paper-Conference.pdf), §§2–3, Definition3.1 and Theorems3.1–3.2, relate policy/Taylor matching to trajectory error under local incremental input-to-state stability and regularity conditions. The required order depends on expert robustness. A small neural Jacobian or imitation loss alone is not their closed-loop stability assumption. The paper also identifies regimes where its sufficient neighborhood becomes impractically small; it supplies no Stage-specific stability constants.

**Local obstruction:** [T667](t667/FINDINGS.md) passed numerical differentiation yet only two of four selected finite-history secants passed its original first-order gate. [T668](t668/FINDINGS.md) found only two of four actual student-state recoveries; no successful labels exist for the other two. These results do not mathematically disprove local delta-ISS, but do not establish the useful neighborhood/teacher robustness needed to transfer the paper's rationale. Arbitrary independent120-column perturbations are not verified physical histories. The record offers no justified higher-order direction set or finite-response neighborhood without another unsupported tuning/diagnostic program.

Earlier results are consistent cautions, not all negatives: [T606R morning report](../overnight_20261116/recovery_001/MORNING_REPORT_20261117.md) records real retention/restoration from31 to33 on its historical49-task set; T607's GEM-style proposal was **not launched** after the parent error. [T608 final report](../day_20261117/t608/FINAL_REPORT.md) records a further historical research improvement, not current384 retention. [T614](../day_20261117/t614/FINDINGS_AND_NEXT.md) preserved four conditional recoveries but lost original starts; [T616](../day_20261117/t616/FINDINGS_AND_NEXT.md) kept parent-history drift below .00025 yet one critical original start still failed. [T631 review](../day_20261118/after_t631/FINDINGS_AND_NEXT.md) separates frozen recovery failure from learning interference; [T632 actual report](../day_20261118/t632/FINAL_REPORT.json) contains64 supervised SGD steps and a gained/lost swap, not universal recovery. T691R's induced histories were local function labels, not successful recovery demonstrations, and produced no admitted native candidate.

**Reject now:** no established finite feedback operator, valid correction source and fixed complete learning comparison were found. Do not tighten proximal bounds, add derivatives, change anchors or retry occupancy BC as a rescue. T674's valid linear KKT solution but failed nonlinear objective gate, [T688 optimizer review](T688_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md), and [T689](t689/FINDINGS.md)'s unsaved-scalar joint pre-CG rejection remain separate limits, not grounds for another projected/SGD/Fisher variant.
