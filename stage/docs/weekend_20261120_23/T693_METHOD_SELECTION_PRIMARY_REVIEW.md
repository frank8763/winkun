The new distinction is thus **the sampling law and derivative target**, not renamed conditional branching: positive/negative derivative measures, known normalization, prefix occupancy sampling, and direct backpropagation into Actor means. ## Primary-source support and limits

[Bhatt, Koppel and Krishnamurthy, arXiv:2004.04843v1, 9 April 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.04843v1), Theorem1, Example1, Algorithms1–2 and §V-C, provide the weak-derivative/Jordan decomposition and initial signed-action interventions followed by the original policy. Their Gaussian example motivates Rayleigh components. Their infinite-horizon discounted analysis, regularity assumptions and pendulum experiment do not establish Stage convergence, empirical variance reduction, deterministic retention, or a safe finite Adam step. The two upper-bound comparisons are not a proof of lower actual variance here. The extracted PDF/HTML displays suspicious missing negative exponent signs; do not copy those density expressions into implementation. No author implementation was audited.

[Schulman et al., ICML2015 published PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/schulman15.pdf), §§5–6, supplies the vine alternative: sampled policy prefixes, multiple actions, Monte Carlo continuation, optional common random numbers, and additional simulator/reset cost. Its practical mean-KL approximation and omitted estimation error limit transfer of monotonic-improvement claims. Neither its theory nor Stage's panel KL certifies each of384 deterministic tasks. Read these sections and their limitation discussion, not every appendix or author implementation.

[Wu, Goodman and Ermon, AISTATS2019 published PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/wu19c/wu19c.pdf), introduction and §2.2, motivates correlated samples with correct marginals; its application is variational inference. It supplies no Stage feedback-covariance result. This was used to assess and reject the antithetic priority, not as evidence of navigation improvement.

The following Stage finite-horizon construction is an explicit derivation/proposal, not a theorem quoted from these papers. Source summaries above are intentionally narrow. No exhaustive search, full original ChatGPT-history reading, or external code reproduction is claimed.

## Exact target and one changed mechanism

Keep the retained single120/GRU64 T501, current command law with sigma=.05, the existing11906 adapter scope, terminal outcome Y=safeGOAL−collision, all reset/goal/time/contact contracts, executed387 reference target and its existing coefficient, fresh Adam settings and .01 panel-KL admission rule. No Value network or Value optimizer. The finite pilot below optimizes an explicitly declared eight-ID development objective, not the407-ID objective; /512 is one fixed common objective scale, never a realized-horizon divisor.

Let H be the actual external causal history before one decision; the Actor reconstructs its **own** carry from H. Let mu(H) be the mean of the existing raw Gaussian command before clipping/quantization. The physical action mapping C includes all existing clipping and float32/command conversion. Future actions follow frozen T501/sigma.05, through their own evolving histories. Define Q(H,u)=E[Y | current command C(u), subsequent pi_sigma]. It is an estimand obtained from actual suffixes, not a learned function.

For coordinate d, sample R with density r exp(−r²/2), r≥0, and independently sample the other coordinate Z_other~N(0,1). Form u_d^+=mu_d+sigma R and u_d^−=mu_d−sigma R, with u_other=mu_other+sigma Z_other shared within the pair. Replay both actual commands C(u^±), then run complete fresh same-policy suffixes. The scalar mean derivative estimator is

    D_d = (Y_d^+ − Y_d^−) / (sigma * sqrt(2*pi)).

This follows by splitting the derivative of the normal density at mu: ∂p_mu(u)/∂mu=(u−mu)p_mu(u)/sigma². Each positive/negative half has mass1/(sigma sqrt(2*pi)); normalizing each gives the stated signed-Rayleigh laws. Integrating any bounded measurable physical return through C preserves the identity: no differentiability of collision labels, clipping, quantization or simulator is needed. Clipped duplicate commands contribute their actual outcomes; do not reject them and redraw to exaggerate an effect. This identity concerns a Gaussian mean; preflight must reject applying it at the wrong post-transform variable.

At fixed H, backpropagate sum_d stop_gradient(D_d)*mu_d(H) through the allowed Actor parameters, including the Actor's history-dependent carry computation, while holding the external observed history fixed. Do not backpropagate through simulator states, terminal Y, or sampling choices. Use the exact detached horizon/sampling coefficient below. This is a gradient estimate for the declared equal-ID stochastic terminal objective at T501, rather than a gradient of deterministic pi_0 success. Finite samples and Adam can still produce a poor update.

**Matched control:** same frozen prefixes, same allowed parameters/reference/Adam, four native suffixes per active prefix. Use two pairs of independently drawn standard-normal two-dimensional current innovations z1,z2. Within a pair share one suffix innovation stream independent of both current actions, and form the vector estimate (Y1−Y2)*(z1−z2)/(2*sigma). Average the two pair estimates. Its cross terms have zero expectation conditional on the shared suffix innovations because the two current innovations are independent and each score has mean zero; this argument does not apply to dependent antithetic current actions. The non-differentiated raw latent command is logged so this Gaussian score remains valid through C. No fitted baseline, advantage standardization, paired-trajectory LOO or changed reward. Control and treatment have four actual suffixes each and matched declared streams where their different laws permit. Common innovations mean neither common future states nor common future commands. Their expected local gradients agree; their finite credit estimates differ. Do not compare a costly MVD proposal solely to historical T678 trained on different data.
