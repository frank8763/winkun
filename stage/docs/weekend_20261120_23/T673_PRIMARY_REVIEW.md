## Estimator and what no-signal means

Keep T501 frozen at its recorded sigma=.05 and the existing 11906 adapter parameters. For task k with n_k=4 nonfield or 8 field trajectories, retain the registered terminal score Y=+1 safe arrival, -1 collision, 0 other, and the fixed positive score scale S. Define

```text
s_ki = sum_t grad_theta log p_theta(a_kit | H_kit) at theta0
b_ki = sum_(j != i) Y_kj / (n_k - 1)
g_k  = sum_i (Y_ki - b_ki) s_ki / (n_k S)
g_407 = sum_(k in all407) g_k / 407
g_field = sum_(k in field24) g_k / 24
```

Within-task trajectories must be independent draws at the same behavior policy and task initialization, with valid likelihoods and regular differentiation/integrability. A detached baseline built from other trajectories is independent of trajectory i; since E[s_ki]=0, its expected score contribution vanishes. Thus this estimates the stochastic episodic outcome gradient/S at theta0. This is our conditional score-function derivation; multiple-sample REINFORCE baselines are established prior art. Kool et al.'s author page confirms that subject, but its linked PDF failed to fetch during this review, so no claim here relies on unread proofs. [Author publication](https://wouterkool.github.io/publication/buy-4-samples-free-baseline/).

Do not divide each episode by its own length: the objective is an episode outcome, and its likelihood score sums decisions. Do not concatenate all trajectories into one flat mean: eight field samples should improve estimation rather than double a task's objective weight. Do not divide g_407 by 50 or g_field by two when skipping exactly zero contributions; that changes their scales relative to the nominal replay term. Do not whiten or normalize each task's advantage without separately declaring that objective change. These are algebraic consequences of the displayed estimator.

For constant observed Y, every LOO advantage is exactly zero. Mark these 357 tasks **sample no-signal**, distinguishing all-safe, all-collision and all-zero. They need no expensive per-trajectory score computation for this terminal-result estimator, but remain in the 407 denominator and in history-change checks. Zero does not mean the true gradient is zero, the task is safe, or a retention constraint is satisfied. In particular, reweighting an all-collision zero estimator cannot create directional information. A mixed sample can still cancel or be dominated by one trajectory. Four/eight samples and selected fixed seeds do not provide reliable rare-event or simultaneous407 confidence guarantees.

Reconstruct the complete causal GRU from reset using recorded external inputs and applied actions, with each evaluated parameter set producing its own hidden carry. Differentiate through that network history, holding simulator observations/actions fixed; truncation or stored behavior hidden states changes the derivative. For independently clipped Gaussian action coordinates, interior likelihood is the Gaussian density, while each endpoint is a probability atom given by the corresponding tail mass. Use recorded atom flags and stable log-tail functions; an endpoint density or a numerical probability floor can give incorrect scores. CAPG explicitly derives tail-probability scores and their unbiasedness/variance properties under its assumptions. It does not validate this repository's implementation. [Fujita and Maeda, §3, Eqs.9–10](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/fujita18a/fujita18a.pdf).

## Conditional KL, accumulated KL and their limits

For every recorded full trajectory and each fixed diagnostic candidate, distinguish maximum per-decision conditional change, episode mean (sum/T), episode sum, equal-episode task mean, and maximum task mean. Keep IDs, time indices, lengths, quantiles and the three forgotten tasks visible. A small episode mean can hide a brief large change; a large sum can also reflect a long horizon rather than one extreme event. T671 already checked maximum task-average KL: merely renaming that statistic adds no evidence. Use sigma from source metadata, as motivated by the [T671 scale correction](t671/POSTHOC_KL_SCALE_CORRECTION.md).

With equal fixed diagonal sigma, latent conditional Gaussian KL is ||mu0(H)-mu1(H)||²/(2 sigma²), summing action dimensions, with each network's own causal carry. Label this **latent conditional KL**. It is not the exact mixed atom/interior applied-action KL; clipping is a measurable map and can only reduce KL. If exact clipped KL is not computed, do not claim it was measured. Likewise, a realized sampled log-ratio can be negative and is not a nonnegative conditional KL.

Our finite-horizon factorization derivation: for the same task/reset distribution, parameter-independent environment kernel, full external history, and an absorbing post-terminal extension,

```text
KL(P0(trajectory) || P1(trajectory))
  = E_(trajectory ~ P0) sum_t KL(p0(.|H_t) || p1(.|H_t)).
```

Transition factors cancel, and conditioning the log action-ratio gives the conditional KL. Thus an average of complete episode sums estimates this forward trajectory KL for a fixed candidate; sums of latent KL instead give an upper-bound expectation. An individual recorded sum is not the exact trajectory-distribution KL. Finite old histories cannot measure all changed-policy occupancy, and selecting a candidate from those same histories further limits statistical inference. The identity supplies neither deterministic `clip(mu)` retention nor a bound at every unseen state. CPO carefully distinguishes its expected-cost/divergence bounds from practical linear/quadratic approximations and sampling error; empirical KL tables or tangent constraints here do not inherit its guarantees. [Achiam et al., §§4–6](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a/achiam17a.pdf).

## Mean, robust objective and stopping interpretation

Task-balanced mean return, worst-task return and per-task nondegradation constraints are different choices. Equal task weights remove sampling-count bias; they do not prevent harm to a rare task. A worst-task or tail-risk objective would deliberately change the objective and needs its own preregistration, estimation support and matched comparison. Contemporary primary work DRATS formulates MTRL through worst return gaps and KL-regularized task sampling; it distinguishes adaptive collection from objective reweighting, and states limitations in reference-return estimation. Its setting/assumptions do not show that changing weights can repair this fixed sparse field batch. [Corrado et al., 2026 preprint, §§4.1–4.3,6](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14350v1).
