## Why stochastic executed labels are valid, and what must change

The actual [Actor step](../../experiments/t496_neural_speed_profile/speed_actor.py) computes causal normalized observation/GRU carry and location, then `raw=location+sigma*z`, then `action=clip(raw).float()`. Noise changes the action and subsequent physical history; it does not directly enter the GRU or120 inputs. The next observation includes the previous executed command. [T665 inspect](../../experiments/t665_remaining_verification/t665_native.py) verifies `actions==clip(raw_actions).astype(float32)`, own causal full history, no ownership and the seeded stochastic flags. Its [frozen audit](../../experiments/t665_remaining_verification/t665_runtime.py) verifies fresh/exact array hashes and integration hashes. [Canonical Session](../../experiments/t651_canonical_outcome_pg/t651_native.py) checks scene, available initial integration/120 contract, goal and zero history before stepping.

Therefore a deterministic candidate can reproduce such a trajectory if it emits each recorded **action**, beginning at decision zero, on its own causal carry. Source `locations`, unclipped `raw_actions`, innovations or a source seed are not the target commands and must not become deployed inputs. No need to reproduce the source's noise or density. Conversely, verifying the noisy source with `sample=False` against its executed actions will usually fail at the first command; that is not evidence of a bad source. Audit the original source with its original sampling/config separately, then reconstruct the common frozen-body own carry on its recorded observations. Fit the deterministic candidate to recorded actions. Complete identical initial state plus identical command/hold schedule gives identical subsequent observations and own carry by induction. This is conditional same-runtime finite physical replay, not a stochastic success-probability guarantee. [Existing exact-command review](EXACT_COMMAND_RETENTION_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md).
