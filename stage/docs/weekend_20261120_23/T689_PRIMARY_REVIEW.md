## What the metric means

For the original384 deterministic success histories, define

`K_R(theta) = mean_ID mean_t [||mu_theta(h_t)-mu_0(h_t)||²/(2*.05²)]`,

`F_R = mean_ID mean_t [J_mu(h_t)^T diag(.05^-2) J_mu(h_t)]` at theta0.

Thus `F_R` is exactly the local Hessian of this finite reference latent-Gaussian KL. It integrates actions analytically, while empirically averaging histories; it is **not** sampled action-score outer-product Fisher. Preserve all cross-parameter terms, even with matrix-free products. No diagonal/block/feature-covariance substitute. Each ID has equal total weight; do not accidentally weight long episodes more. The three extra acquisition references affect the unchanged loss but not this predeclared384-history metric or matching set.

[Kakade, NIPS2001, §2 Eqs.2–4](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/2073-a-natural-policy-gradient.pdf) defines policy geometry using action Fisher averaged by the policy's state distribution. Here successful deterministic reference histories differ from current stochastic occupancy, and the loss includes supervised retention. Call this **damped reference-distribution Gaussian natural-gradient geometry**, not ordinary on-policy NPG or a TRPO reproduction. Adding `.1 I` also introduces coordinate-dependent Euclidean geometry, so exact reparameterization invariance is not claimed.

[TRPO, ICML2015, §§3–7](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/schulman15.pdf) supports analytic action-integrated KL curvature and practical CG, but distinguishes theoretical maximum-divergence conditions from its average-KL approximation. Its practical algorithm also checks objective improvement by line search. T689 instead matches a fixed historical control KL; no monotonic-return theorem transfers. Its tasks are simulated locomotion/Atari, not this recurrent navigation retention problem.

Clipping produces an interior density plus boundary atoms. By the deterministic data-processing inequality, clipped-command KL at a **fixed same history** is at most latent Gaussian KL; their Fishers need not agree. This upper bound does not turn mean conditional KL into deterministic mode retention, a trajectory-KL bound on the candidate's occupancy, or a guarantee on new histories. Do not compute `J_clip(mu)` and call it Gaussian Fisher. T683's correlated conditional-atom caveat remains separate. [T683 findings](t683/FINDINGS.md).
