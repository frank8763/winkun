Primary support is [Greensmith–Bartlett–Baxter, JMLR2004, Theorems13/16](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume5/greensmith04a/greensmith04a.pdf), and [Chung et al., ICML2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/chung21a/chung21a.pdf), including the trajectory REINFORCE derivation in [arXiv v3 Appendix D.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.13773v3#page=36). Greensmith's GPOMDP result is not itself an episodic theorem. For full trajectory score `s`, `E[s]=0` and finite required moments, minimizing covariance trace of `(Y-b)s` gives `b*=E[Y||s||²]/E[||s||²]` and `V(b)=V(b*)+E[||s||²](b-b*)²`. Ordinary mean return need not be optimal. Chung also shows lower variance need not improve optimization.

The proposed small-sample peer ratio is not the population optimum and need not reduce variance. Independent peers imply `E[b_i*s_i]=0`, preserving the **initial raw PG expectation**, not the finite clipped-PPO function or Adam update. LOO contributions share peers: empirical contribution dispersion is not an unbiased batch-gradient variance estimator. No variance gate.
