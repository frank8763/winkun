# T507 joint success supervision: primary sources and limits

Date: 2026-11-12. Research note only; no experiments or runtime changes performed by this review. Sources below were opened directly. Full text was inspected for policy distillation, DAgger, progressive networks and CAPG; SIL and Implicit Behavioral Cloning claims below are limited to their publisher abstracts. This is a bounded engineering pilot, not an originality claim or an isolated causal test.

## Verified primary literature

| Primary source | Evidence and applicable limit |
|---|---|
| Rusu et al., **Policy Distillation**, 2015/2016, §§3–4. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.06295v2). | Supervised policy transfer and consolidation of multiple policies were demonstrated with Atari Q-network teachers. This motivates joint action supervision, but does not establish that continuous executed-action supervision improves this Stage policy or preserves every task. T507 does not reproduce their teacher/Q-distribution objective. |
| Oh et al., **Self-Imitation Learning**, ICML 2018. [Publisher abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/oh18b.html). | SIL learns from past good decisions through an off-policy actor-critic algorithm and reports Atari and MuJoCo results. Reusing the agent's own successes is established prior art. T507 has no new value learning or on-policy RL and is not an implementation of SIL. |
| Ross, Gordon and Bagnell, **A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning**, AISTATS 2011, introduction and Algorithm 3.1. [Full paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a/ross11a.pdf). | Actions affect later observations; supervised error under demonstration states can compound after deployment. DAgger aggregates expert labels at learner-visited states, with explicit reduction assumptions. Fixed success-history supervision without such expert queries does not inherit its guarantee and must be checked in native closed loop. |
| Florence et al., **Implicit Behavioral Cloning**, CoRL 2021 / PMLR 2022. [Publisher abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v164/florence22a.html). | The paper studies complex, discontinuous and multi-valued behavior and reports advantages of implicit policies over common explicit policies. It supports treating multimodal supervision as a real concern; it does not establish that T507 needs an implicit policy or any new architecture. |
| Fujita and Maeda, **Clipped Action Policy Gradient**, ICML 2018, §§2–3. [Full paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/fujita18a/fujita18a.pdf). | Clipping changes executed-action semantics: all raw samples beyond a bound execute the same bounded action. CAPG exploits this for a policy-gradient estimator. It does not prove the proposed supervised boundary hinge is unbiased or improves deterministic navigation. |
| Rusu et al., **Progressive Neural Networks**, 2016, §2. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.04671). | Freezing task columns preserves their functions while later tasks use added columns and lateral connections. T507 keeps its existing architecture and does not implement progressive networks. More generally, freezing a parent while adding a globally active residual would not preserve the composed policy's old-task behavior; the progressive-network forgetting claim cannot be borrowed for that design. |

The fixed objective jointly uses (a) all twelve actual T506 successful histories with float32 executed/clipped-action boundary-hinge supervision, balanced equally across the three families and four episodes per family, and (b) fifteen actual accepted-T505 old8+field7 successful histories with untrimmed locations and their unclipped retention semantics. For the stochastic repair histories, each loss is half the full physical-time mean plus half the physical-time mean on actual goal distance <=1m; do not apply a top-20% loss term that could emphasize squared exploration-noise outliers. For each retention history, use half the physical-time mean plus half the top-20% risk term. The 1m threshold is an offline sample-weighting rule only; it changes neither the native success radius nor runtime behavior and is not a waypoint. These weights are engineering choices without a paper-derived performance guarantee. Do not replace actual histories with synthetic terminal states, mix raw and executed action targets, or discard original recurrent history. Initial replay/density checks must verify the same accepted T505 policy, the twelve stored log-probability records and true cached histories under the declared action semantics.

At most three blocks of 32 supervised Adam steps are allowed. Each proposed block receives 18 native cases (old8+field7+three new task IDs) and 18 exact replays. The original fifteen IDs are protected from the outset; newly qualified successful IDs join that set. Stop on the first rejected block with full Actor+Adam rollback; stop early if all eighteen are safe. Exact replays verify reproducibility and are not independent samples.

If at least one new task has a qualified success, finish the remaining fourteen field cases and all twenty-four exact field replays. The final ten field cases already checked by the same-policy guard are explicitly reused. Final retention requires the old8 and every original field7 success to remain safe, more than seven field successes, and at most ten contacts; protected IDs, not only counts, control acceptance. Missing, invalid or censored tests are not passes. The registered plan/budget and verified final artifacts control execution if wording differs from this note.

## Why success supervision may still fail

The following are project-specific deductions, not paper guarantees. Twelve successes at the same sample count establish usable successful trajectories; they do not establish that a supervised update will improve the deterministic mean policy. Exploration changes both actions and visited states. Regressing successful noisy actions can average incompatible local choices; under squared loss the conditional mean can lie between successful modes and itself fail. Family balancing prevents a long family from automatically dominating, but does not remove contradictory labels, partial observability, or closed-loop distribution shift. No multimodal architecture change is authorized in this pilot.

For a clipped action at the upper bound, the raw action is only known to be at or above that bound; the analogous lower-bound label specifies a half-line. A one-sided hinge therefore avoids treating a censored endpoint as a uniquely identified raw latent target. Interior executed actions have ordinary matching semantics. This is an engineering loss choice, not maximum likelihood for a censored Gaussian, a recovered exploration-free teacher, or CAPG. It does not recover the unknown optimal deterministic action from a successful sample. Preserve actual float32 labels and the precise clipping/boundary tolerance in the registered implementation.

Finite native per-ID guards empirically test accepted block endpoints on the registered starts. They can detect losses that offline action fit misses; they are not global retention, safety, stability, or unseen-start guarantees, and do not test intermediate Adam states. The development IDs influence model selection. This pilot changes the supervision data and objective together relative to T506 and does not isolate capacity, optimizer, learning-rate, or algorithm effects.

A rejection or no new qualified success means this fixed joint-supervision pilot did not satisfy its finite acceptance contract. It does not prove the tasks unreachable, the architecture incapable, success replay useless, or further RL impossible. A pass would show bounded empirical improvement on this development panel only; complete old275, wider-distance and unseen-start retention remain untested here. No automatic grid, repeat launch, budget extension or next experiment follows from either outcome.
