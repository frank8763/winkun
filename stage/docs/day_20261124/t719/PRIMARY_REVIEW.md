## Primary methods and author implementation

[AWR, Peng et al., 2019, author PDF](https://xbpeng.github.io/projects/AWR/AWR_2019.pdf), equations2/10/15–16, Algorithm1, sections3.3/5.2: fits a value baseline then maximizes action log likelihood weighted by exponentiated estimated advantage. Off-policy replay is explicit. Its no-baseline ablation is RWR; removing the baseline hurts performance in reported benchmarks. Thus omitting the baseline is a real departure, not an equivalent AWR implementation. The paper's state/return weighting and approximate policy-improvement derivation do not establish improvement for our per-trajectory outcome score.

Read author [learning/awr_agent.py](https://github.com/xbpeng/awr/blob/831442fb8d4c24bd200667cbc5e458c7657effc2/learning/awr_agent.py), master revision `831442fb8d4c24bd200667cbc5e458c7657effc2`: lines160–168 implement weighted log likelihood plus an action-bound penalty;396–415 subtract values, normalize advantages, exponentiate by temperature, then cap weights. This code is evidence for the distinction above, not a source to transplant unchanged: its Gaussian/bounds handling is not Stage's executed-command censored likelihood. Repository lists MIT license; no third-party code was copied here.

[SIL, Oh et al., ICML2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/oh18b/oh18b.pdf), equations1–3, uses `max(R-V,0)` for replay policy/value losses, alongside online actor-critic learning. It can give zero weight to some samples; it does not mean simply selecting safe episodes. The introduction itself warns that repeating proximal distractor rewards can hurt learning. Stage's all-positive, critic-free, fixed-batch outcome fit is not canonical SIL.

Read author [baselines/common/self_imitation.py](https://github.com/junhyukoh/self-imitation-learning/blob/13eb8a79e9585f92761e0a4670bd76c2e0a7bf05/baselines/common/self_imitation.py), lines323–337: clipped positive return-minus-value drives policy and value updates. Author [PPO integration](https://github.com/junhyukoh/self-imitation-learning/blob/13eb8a79e9585f92761e0a4670bd76c2e0a7bf05/baselines/ppo2/ppo2_sil.py), lines70–83 and219–231, adds SIL to PPO. Master revision `13eb8a79e9585f92761e0a4670bd76c2e0a7bf05`; repository lists MIT. No code copied.

## A precise eligible empirical objective

Call the proposed method **outcome-weighted maximum likelihood on all72 historical suffixes**, an engineering offline pilot in the RWR/weighted-imitation family. Do not call it new AWR, exact AWR/SIL, unbiased policy gradient, continued on-policy PPO, or theoretical safe policy improvement.

For ID i and its six episodes j, freeze score `q_ij=+1` for actual SAFE, `-1` for any collision, otherwise0 (including timeout and boundary-overlap non-safe). Assert SAFE and collision cannot both hold; collision takes precedence in a corrupted/conflicting label and admission must fail rather than silently relabel. Freeze beta1 and `w_ij=exp(q_ij)/sum_j exp(q_ij)`. Every episode has strictly positive weight; safe:neutral:collision unnormalized weights are e:1:1/e, safe/collision ratio e². Neutral boundary outcomes remain failures in reports, even though the loss assigns score0. These are outcome labels, not the unchanged native environment reward or discounted return-to-go.

Minimize `L=-(1/12) sum_i sum_j w_ij (1/T_ij) sum_{t in suffix_ij} log p_theta(a_executed|historical observation and own causal carry)`. Require `T_ij>0`. Equal ID mass and equal per-trajectory time averaging are explicit: without `1/T_ij`, long timeouts gain extra mass and the intended experiment changes. This normalization is a deliberate empirical weighting, not a claim of exact trajectory-level EM or AWR discounted occupancy optimization.

Use every audited training episode once as a data member, never exact repeats as extra members, never just successful/best trajectories. Fixed weights never depend on current learner likelihood, loss or validation. Start from immutable B0, train only the same20198 head, frozen GRU and sigma.2; choose exactly16 full-batch Adam steps at3e-4 before launch, last step only. No choice between16/24 after observation, no teacherT716, no Value/PPO/old403 loss, no new acquisition collection, no extra head or seed search. Audits must reproduce all16 Adam states and final model and assert frozen inputs/body and strictly positive72 weights. Censored command likelihood must retain clipping boundary atoms and existing float32 execution conventions; ordinary Gaussian MSE on clipped targets is not equivalent.

Historical suffixes were on-policy when T717 collected them, but are off-policy for T719, including after the first update. Replaying frozen causal features is legitimate empirical training because the body is frozen; it does not make histories samples from the new learner's state distribution. Prefix is reconstruction only, excluded from fit. Raw old density is provenance, not an importance correction that makes this loss an unbiased gradient of native expected reward.

## Negative evidence, scope and decision

[Wang, Jin & Montana, Learning on One Mode, arXiv2412.03258v2, 19Mar2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.03258v2), sections1/6.2 and FetchReach example, shows conflicting behavioral modes can make unimodal weighted imitation generate unsupported intermediate actions. This is a plausible failure case here, not proof this Stage dataset has conflicting modes. Do not add GMM/mode selection as an unregistered rescue.

[Ross, Gordon & Bagnell, AISTATS2011](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a/ross11a.pdf), introduction, explains that action-induced state-distribution shift can compound imitation errors. Consequently lower frozen-data loss or high historical safe count cannot certify new closed-loop safety. Even positive weights on failures still imitate those actions with lower strength; outcome weighting gives no per-action causal credit assignment.

All72 positive-weight use avoids T717's literal prohibited **successful-subset** rescue, but it remains follow-up learning from a failed experiment and must be described candidly. T717 failure stays unchanged. The new user continuation plus a separately locked objective can support one independent T719; renaming selected-success cloning or continuing T717 would not. Six validation IDs stay outside fitting/weight construction; they are already exposed inventory and no longer pristine generalization evidence. Never select steps/beta/scores by these results.

Scientifically acceptable as one bounded empirical pilot, with no mechanism/novelty claim, only if source/input/admission/preflight succeed.
