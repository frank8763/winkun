## Why deterministic failure and stochastic performance are distinct

PPO's gradient and surrogate use actions sampled from a stochastic policy; its paper's Section2 and Algorithm1 alternate such collection with optimization. This objective does not assert that executing a Gaussian location at every decision inherits the stochastic policy's return. OpenAI's own PPO actor samples `pi.sample()` in `MLPActorCritic.step`; Baselines explicitly implements Gaussian `mode()` as mean and `sample()` as mean plus scaled Gaussian noise. Thus stochastic evaluation is a legitimate measurement of the trained policy, and deterministic evaluation measures a different action rule. [PPO paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.06347), [OpenAI PPO actor source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/spinningup/master/spinup/algos/pytorch/ppo/core.py), [OpenAI Baselines distribution source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/baselines/master/baselines/common/distributions.py).

For this repository call the deterministic rule **clipped location**, not the mathematical mode or expectation of the censored command distribution: boundary atoms and float32 rounding make that distinction material. In nonlinear closed-loop navigation, the trajectory generated by mean commands need not have the expected return of sampled trajectories; this is a consequence of evaluating a different policy, not evidence of an implementation error or proof that exploration is beneficial. PPO optimizes its declared reward surrogate, not a guarantee of safe-GOAL probability. A correct update can improve neither metric. Ilyas et al. document cases where surrogate improvement conflicts with actual return, including PPO; read Section2.3/Figures6–7 rather than relying on the abstract. [A Closer Look at Deep Policy Gradients](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.02553).

The essential randomized control is initial sigma .2 versus final sigma .2. Comparing final stochastic success only to initial/final deterministic outcomes confounds changed mean parameters with added random actions. Reusing old deterministic results by exact source/model SHA is appropriate contextual evidence and requires zero reruns. Even if both stochastic arms beat their own clipped-location policies, that only supports a sampling effect on these fixed conditions. If final exceeds initial under matched noise, it supports a local trained-parameter effect; it does not identify which PPO component caused it or prove robust recovery.

## Common random numbers: couple noise, preserve each policy's causal path

Use a distinct fixed seed stream for each `(condition ID, replicate)` and use the same stream in both arms. Couple standardized iid epsilon by **suffix decision index and action coordinate**, not raw action, clipped command, state, physical time or terminal length: `u_m,t=mu_m(history_m,t)+.2*epsilon_ID,rep,t`. Each policy then executes its own float32 clipped command, observes its own resulting state, carries its own GRU, and ends at its own genuine terminal event within the same remaining original horizon. No policy borrows the other arm's suffix states, carry or commands. No RNG consumption in the forced prefix, inspection, scheduling or another job may shift the paired suffix stream. If implementation does consume draws in prefix, verify identical suffix-index alignment explicitly rather than assuming equal seeds establish it. Record seed, model/source hashes, raw/atom/command data, own histories and array/integration hashes.

Common random numbers are a coupling intended to sharpen comparisons, not new tasks or extra independent worlds. Glasserman and Yao explicitly discuss matching corresponding random variables and the conditions needed for variance reduction; reduction is not automatic. Here policy state paths diverge and clipped nonlinear dynamics offer no such guarantee. Verify the intended epsilon correspondence on the overlapping suffix, including saturated commands by retained raw variables; do not infer it from equal terminal scores or clipped actions. [Glasserman and Yao, Some Guidelines and Guarantees for Common Random Numbers, Sections2.3–2.4](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/4261/glasserman_yao_guidelines.pdf).

## Paired reporting and uncertainty

Primary outcome is the existing safe-GOAL indicator. Keep collisions, timeouts and boundary overlaps individually visible, with remaining distance, native steps and declared return as secondary measurements. Do not replace arrival with improved return or terminal distance. Report every ID and all three replicate pairs, including gain/loss/tie counts and training/validation membership. All 18 conditions were already exposed; the six are held out only from T717 optimizer updates.

For outcome `Y`, define `d_i=(1/3)*sum_r(Y_final,i,r-Y_initial,i,r)`. Report each `d_i`, overall `mean_18(d_i)`, training `mean_12`, and heldout `mean_6`; provide both arms' denominators54/36/18 draws with the accompanying **18/12/6 condition clusters**. A draw-level paired2×2 table is useful descriptively, but an ordinary54-pair McNemar/binomial/Wilson analysis as if these were independent task observations is unsuitable. Audit repeats never enter these tables. The three new evaluation seeds are not three independent training seeds; this remains one learned model from one training run. Primary evaluation research warns that few-run aggregate point estimates hide material uncertainty. [Agarwal et al., Deep RL at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice](https://research.google/pubs/deep-reinforcement-learning-at-the-edge-of-the-statistical-precipice/), [author implementation](https://github.com/google-research/rliable).

If uncertainty summaries are added, resample whole paired ID clusters with all three replicates and both models kept together, separately respecting12/6 strata, and label the interval an exploratory **condition-resampling sensitivity analysis**. These purposively selected same-map conditions are not an iid draw from unseen worlds; even18-cluster resampling cannot establish such population inference. Six heldout clusters and three noise draws per ID have low resolution. A sign-flip/permutation p-value requires an explicit exchangeability/random-assignment assumption absent from merely evaluating two fixed checkpoints; do not present one as an exact causal test. A Monte Carlo noise estimate over this fixed panel, an assumption-based condition interval, and training-seed generality are different claims.

## Scoped decision gate

Recommended scientific interpretation is descriptive, with no automatic qualification: require a complete108-fresh/108-exact panel, all model/prefix/own-carry/noise/density/native audits, positive overall safe difference **and** positive six-ID heldout safe difference, and no increased collision or boundary count overall or in heldout, before calling the result a **local stochastic improvement worth separately confirming**. This practical gate is a conservative recommendation, not a paper-derived significance threshold; register it before outcomes and do not retrofit it. Report all metrics even if the gate fails. Train-only gain, more reward without safe-GOAL gain, equal success with more collisions, or a few lucky new seeds is not acquisition evidence. Passing this small gate still does not pass T717's failed deterministic acquisition gate or authorize integration/deployment.

A null or negative result means this fixed trained stochastic checkpoint has not demonstrated an advantage over the matched initial exploration control under the bounded evaluation. It does not prove exploration alone explains every prior success, that PPO cannot learn recovery, or that the frozen representation is sufficient/insufficient. A positive result supplies narrow evidence about these two fixed distributions on these18 exposed conditional histories. Neither outcome justifies adding variance/seed/checkpoint searches tonight. No unseen-map, independent-world, algorithmic-novelty or original-start claim follows.
