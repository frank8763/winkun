## Decision and scope

One fixed successful-behavior augmentation comparison is reasonable. It addresses a data-support hypothesis that the completed weight grid did not test. T712 did not improve the declared retention/development tradeoff over B0; that finite result does not show weighting is universally ineffective. T713 gave B030/72 versus compact28 and parent27, with28 B0 collisions; parent and compact successes were subsets of B0 successes. Thus T713 supplies no demonstrated recovery source for B0 failures and must not be mined for one. [T712](t712/FINDINGS.md), [T713](t713/FINDINGS.md)

Successful autonomous trajectories on identical initial tasks establish that those source policies solved those cases. They do **not** establish expert recovery from B0's different intermediate states. Training on their complete histories changes both occupancy and targets. Call this finite supervised behavior augmentation, not label-only correction, new RL, DAgger, DART, or a proven new navigation algorithm. The earlier T696/T698 line already integrated autonomous successful behavior through finite supervised expansion; the difference here is a fixed small deployment head and a matched training comparison, not invention of successful-trajectory supervision. ## Primary sources and actual reading

- [DAgger, Ross et al.2011, primary abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) describes policy-dependent visitation and an iterative no-regret reduction. T714 uses a fixed historical success pool and does not iteratively label the trained learner's visits; its guarantees do not transfer.
- [DART, Laskey et al.2017, primary abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/laskey17a.html) collects demonstrations under deliberate supervisor disturbances to mitigate covariate shift. T714 does not collect disturbed-supervisor corrections or optimize a noise model. Nominal successful histories are not evidence of recovery competence on arbitrary B0 histories.
- The [DART author README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BerkeleyAutomation/DART/master/README.md) identifies separate BC, DAgger and noise-injection experiments. Only the README was inspected; three guessed root-level test-script paths returned404, so no DART/DAgger executable-source reproduction is claimed.
- The maintained [imitation BC source, module description and BehaviorCloningLossCalculator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HumanCompatibleAI/imitation/master/src/imitation/algorithms/bc.py) implements supervised observation/action learning and evaluates supplied actions under the policy. Its loss is negative log likelihood with optional terms, not Stage's deterministic residual MSE. This supports the classification as BC-style supervision, not numerical equivalence to that implementation. Mutable master code was inspected, not executed or copied.

These sources motivate the question and its limitations; none certifies Stage success or novelty. This review ends with these sources rather than a new literature queue.

The proposed new label is `executed_command_float64 - parent_raw_mean`. Executed commands have already passed clipping and float32 conversion. This label is a command-canonical residual, not generally the producer's raw residual. For self-replay, B0 at initialization can therefore have nonzero raw-residual MSE and gradient even though its executed commands already match perfectly; saturated coordinates are the clearest example. Report clipping/saturation counts, initial raw residual MSE and executed-command mismatch counts. Do not call self-command replay a no-op or exact raw-output-preserving control. Keep the old403 zero-residual targets and existing7 source targets unchanged in both arms; do not silently canonicalize historical old targets too.

The source command identity audit is not an admission requirement that the initial B0 head already fits the successful source. That mismatch is the intended learning target. Source success must not be reinterpreted as every state/action being optimal or universally safe.

## Choose one control and state the estimand

**Preferred simpler comparison:** control uses the existing nominal objective `.5*old403 + .5*existing7`; treatment uses `.5*old403 + .25*existing7 + .25*new_successes`. Both start from B0 with identical optimizer, tolerances, chunk order,80 closure cap/64 attempt cap and no extra ridge or rescue. This directly tests replacing half the source supervision budget with audited successful behavior. It changes source weighting, occupancy, labels and data volume together; it is not a pure effect of better labels. Existing unrefitted B0 is a third reference, not the matched extra-training control. Reuse an exactly identical completed T712 replay50 only if every state/optimizer/data/arithmetic contract matches and explicitly label that historical computation; otherwise do not claim equivalence.

**If the proposed self-history control is retained instead:** use `.5*old403 + .25*existing7 + .25*B0_sameID_histories` versus the corresponding successful histories. This better matches task IDs and category mass, but successful versus failed visits, trajectory lengths and command canonicalization still differ. It tests the complete behavior/occupancy package. Failed B0 actions are preservation targets, not expert corrections; keeping them can limit improvement by construction. The control must not be described as equally informative successful data or a universally strongest BC baseline.

Freeze one of these alternatives before fitting; this review does not authorize both. For either design, each category loss should mean equally weighted IDs, each ID's mean over its entire decision sequence and both action coordinates. For category mass `w`, row weight is `w/(2*N_ID*T_ID)`. Unequal trajectory lengths then do not change ID mass, but do change actual forward/backward cost. Report decision counts, closures, completed optimizer steps, wall time and memory; equal closure counts are not equal FLOPs. No truncation or resampling to equalize cost after seeing performance.

Export the last completed optimizer-step state before shadow work, with rollback for partial line searches and deadline exceptions. Provisional exports remain ineligible until independent reconstruction and audit pass. Evaluate both completed arms regardless of forgetting or source successes; do not hide one through a zero-forgetting screen. No weight/seed/epoch/optimizer sweep follows failure.
