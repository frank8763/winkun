## Targeted primary sources and counterevidence

[Ross, Gordon and Bagnell, AISTATS 2011, paper introduction](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a/ross11a.pdf) explains that learned actions change subsequent observation distributions and errors can compound under the learner's own execution. This supports evaluating closed-loop outcomes over the entire fixed start distribution after imitation, rather than treating fitted-data loss or the chosen successful IDs as navigation capability. This is a motivation, not proof that distribution shift caused T719's failures. T720 performs no DAgger collection or expert querying and inherits none of its guarantees. Reviewed introduction and method framing, not an exhaustive paper survey.

[Lopez-Paz and Ranzato, GEM, 2017/author repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/GradientEpisodicMemory) separates final performance from transfer across tasks. The actual [author metrics implementation, `metrics/metrics.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/GradientEpisodicMemory/master/metrics/metrics.py), inspected lines 22–55, stores a result matrix, computes final accuracy and differences against earlier task accuracies, and reports them separately. Thus aggregate performance and loss of previously successful IDs answer different questions. Stage should report paired per-ID gains/losses alongside totals; this is an evaluation adaptation, not GEM training, its benchmark reproduction, or formal GEM backward-transfer measurement on a task sequence. The repository identifies Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licensing; no source code is copied. The retrieved source was the archived repository's `master` on this review date; an immutable commit lookup was unavailable through the browser API, so no pinned-revision claim is made.

[PyTorch official reproducibility notes, version 2.15](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/notes/randomness.html), reproducibility and randomness sections, say equal seeds do not guarantee equality across releases, platforms or CPU/GPU, and discuss controlling randomness and nondeterministic operations. Consequently checkpoint equality alone is insufficient evidence that two evaluations are the same job. Historical reuse below is a local, auditable evidence-identity rule; PyTorch does not prescribe Stage's reuse contract or guarantee its simulator. Do not migrate runtime/backend while claiming exact equivalence.
