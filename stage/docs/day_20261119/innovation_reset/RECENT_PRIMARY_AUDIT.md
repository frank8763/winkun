# Primary-source audit: retaining control outcomes during continual RL

2026-11-19. Research only: **no simulator, optimizer, GPU computation, training, launch, installation or data download**. The 17:00 all-stop has passed. This is a bounded international search and selected-passage audit, not an exhaustive global novelty certificate. Dates, versions and actual reading scope appear beside the sources below. No Phase evidence was consulted.

## Decision

There is no basis yet for calling the proposed mechanism genuinely new. Its constituent ideas have close precedents: preserving many safe actions, policy-conditioned reach-avoid sets, parameter-conditioned value gradients, and constrained policy updates. The useful research question is narrower: can **candidate-continuation outcome differences** protect all previously acquired goal-reaching capabilities while allowing a single direct actor to learn new ones, more efficiently than replay or direct paired rollout acceptance? A new name or combination does not answer that question.

The local motivation is specific. [T656 findings](../t656/FINDINGS_AND_NEXT.md) report field8/24 with old26 repaired and old02 newly lost; old00/03 remain failures. Recorded-history KL reached only 1.13e-5. Its keep-loss weighting gave each field history 48 times one old history's coefficient, and five duplicate IDs had differing successful action targets. These establish limitations of this implementation and its action proxy; they do not establish task incompatibility, intrinsic capacity failure or universal replay failure. [Method assessment](../t656/METHOD_ASSESSMENT.md) also identifies stale sampled histories and stochastic-suffix versus deterministic original-start objective gaps.

## Recent continual-learning evidence

- **Plasticity is not forgetting.** [Dohare et al., Nature, published 21 August 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07711-7), publication extracts on plasticity and PPO/Ant experiments: previously acquired performance can fall while ability to relearn also deteriorates; neuron replacement addresses the latter. This does not diagnose Stage from eight adapter updates. [Wang et al., arXiv:2605.09044v1, 9 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09044v1), abstract/introduction and stated counterexample scope, additionally show representation/NTK rank need not predict trainability in regression/classification. These are counterexamples to universal diagnostic claims, not validation of their replacement metric for Stage navigation.

- **C-CHAIN is verified prior art.** [Tang et al., ICML 2025, PMLR267](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/tang25g.html); [arXiv v1, 31 May 2025, §4.3/Algorithm1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.00592v1) suppresses output churn on reference batches alongside RL. Its [author driver](https://github.com/bluecontra/C-CHAIN/blob/2f8bedfefb6a0a276d7709447a5cdb75cecfdaad/crl_gym_classic_control/control_train_aligned_c_chain.py) and [loss implementation, lines129–218](https://github.com/bluecontra/C-CHAIN/blob/2f8bedfefb6a0a276d7709447a5cdb75cecfdaad/crl_gym_classic_control/basic_utils.py#L129) were inspected at commit `2f8bedfefb6a0a276d7709447a5cdb75cecfdaad`: historical policy cross-entropy, separate reference data and adaptive coefficient are concrete. This threatens novelty in output-response retention or adaptive regularization. It does not certify each task's deterministic closed-loop outcome.

- **Replay plus critic is insufficient by assumption.** [RECALL, TMLR2023; arXiv:2311.11557v1, 20 November 2023, introduction/§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.11557v1) reports failures even with perfect memory, identifying reward-scale imbalance and offline distribution shift. Its response combines target normalization and policy distillation. Both actor and critic explicitly have task-specific heads; its benchmark success cannot be transferred to Stage's prohibited task-ID heads. This older source is included because it directly falsifies “retain every transition, therefore retain every capability.”

- **CIDER is verified, but remains action distillation.** [Li et al., arXiv:2608.21899v1, 22 August 2026, §3.2–4, §5.3/6 and deployment appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21899v1): one shared actor, current-task HIL actor–critic learning, historical-state reverse-KL distillation, and asymmetric adapter-gradient routing. Historical transitions are deliberately excluded from Bellman learning. Its short-horizon manipulation tasks use demonstrations/human corrections; longer sparse horizons remain a limitation. Therefore neither “single actor plus retention” nor avoiding old-transition critic updates is new. Stage has no qualified correcting teacher at its three tested deviations. No author implementation was independently located in the inspected paper/search; this is a method audit, not a code reproduction.

- **Separating dynamics memory from policy memory is established.** [ARROW, arXiv:2603.11395v2, 17 May 2026, §1.4/3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11395v2) replays FIFO plus reservoir-style distribution-matching chunks into a DreamerV3 world model, then trains actor–critic on imagined rollouts without task IDs. It explicitly describes its contribution as a working recipe. Its [paper-linked author repository](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/ARROW-B6F2/) exposed no readable files through this tool; implementation was not verified. A learned dynamics model may retain valid transition information while old Q labels become policy-dependent, but changing only replay destination would reproduce an established direction and introduce model-error validation costs.

- **Resetting and consolidating is another recipe.** [Reset & Distill, arXiv:2403.05066v2, 14 August 2024, abstract/introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.05066v2) distinguishes negative transfer from the plasticity explanation and proposes resetting the online actor/critic, then distilling into an offline actor. This offers a necessary competing explanation for poor new-task learning. Its mechanism is not evidence that another distillation stage will solve Stage's fragile closed loop; author code was not inspected here.

- **Robotic lifelong learning is also established.** [LEGION, Nature Machine Intelligence, 5 February 2025, publication metadata/code-availability extracts](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-00983-2) and its [official repository README/tree](https://github.com/Ghiara/LEGION), unpinned retrieval on this date, identify a language-embedding and non-parametric Bayesian knowledge framework. This is relevant precedent for knowledge preservation/composition, not proof of compatibility with Stage's fixed input contract. Full publisher methods were inaccessible; no architectural or performance claim beyond that inspected scope is used.

## Closest outcome-set and update precedents

- [SafeAdapt, arXiv:2604.09452v1, 10 April 2026, §4.2–4.5 and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09452v1) allows multiple safe actions and projects arbitrary RL updates into an IBP-certified parameter orthotope. Thus “retain a safe region instead of one action” is already close prior art. Its finite discrete state/action spaces, exhaustive safety-critical coverage, evaluable unsafety predicate and greedy selection are material assumptions. It certifies safety under those assumptions, not Stage's continuous recurrent finite-horizon goal success. [Author repository](https://github.com/maxanisimov/provably-safe-policy-updates) was checked at README/tree scope only, unpinned retrieval on this date.

- [Hsu et al., RSS2021, 12–16 July 2021, author manuscript](https://kaichiehhsu.github.io/files/RSS21_RARL.pdf), abstract/formulation scope, derives reach-avoid Bellman learning; neural approximations obtain safety guarantees through a supervisory framework treating them as untrusted. [ABS, arXiv:2401.17583v2, 17 May 2024, §III-B/§V–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.17583v2) is closer still: a policy-conditioned reach-avoid set and discounted backup, trained from fixed agile-policy trajectories, then used for recovery switching. Its reported RA data collection is 200,000 episodes. [Author README](https://github.com/LeCAR-Lab/ABS), current retrieval, confirms deployed agile/recovery/value modules. Neither runtime supervision nor its data budget fits Stage's proposed single-actor setting; the underlying outcome-set idea remains prior art.

- [Persistent Safety Set Guided Offline Safe RL, IJCAI2026, August, §§2–5](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/470) learns CBF-related persistent safety sets and blocks unsafe reward-value propagation. Its set means that **some policy exists** to remain safe indefinitely. That differs from the proposed actor actually reaching the goal before the deadline. The paper's linked GDCBF site was inaccessible here; author code was not audited.

- [RADS, arXiv:2603.00140v2, revised 11 November 2026, §4.2–4.4/AppendixB.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00140v2) already constrains RL using a policy-conditioned minimum-future safety critic. It explicitly distinguishes uncontrolled failure labels from controlled continuation outcomes. Its domain and surrogate are diffusion memorization, not navigation, but merely attaching policy identity to a safety value is not new.

- [Bohlinger/Peters, arXiv:2502.11949v1, 17 February 2025, §2/Algorithm1](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11949v1) learns explicit policy-parameter-conditioned values from whole-policy returns and differentiates them with respect to policy parameters. A scalar family `π(θ+αd)` is a restricted case. [TRPO, arXiv v5, 20 April 2017, §2/AppendixC](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.05477v5) distinguishes candidate occupancy from old-policy surrogates and backtracks along a fixed direction to improve the nonlinear surrogate while satisfying the KL constraint. [CPO, ICML2017, 6–11 August, publication abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a.html) establishes constrained updates as another prior. These do not certify a learned Stage acceptance margin.

- [TaSIL, arXiv:2205.14812v2, 16 January 2023, §3 and finite-difference discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.14812v2) already connects derivative matching to closed-loop imitation error under incremental stability and regularity assumptions. Thus “retain feedback sensitivity instead of only nominal actions” is also established. Stage has not established those assumptions; independently perturbing correlated sensor columns does not create a physically valid state derivative. This is a novelty threat and conditional theoretical reference, not a proposed extra loss.

## One candidate worth deriving, not declaring novel

Freeze an RL-proposed direction `d`; evaluate the **entire** candidate policy family `π(θ+αd)`. Define a difference in finite-horizon reach-avoid capability, `ΔV(h,τ,α)`, relative to the retained actor. Retention would constrain candidate continuation outcomes, allowing alternative successful actions. Training-time counterexamples could select physically valid histories where paired old/candidate trajectories diverge and threaten goal attainment. Deployment remains the same 120-input GRU direct actor, with no value-based runtime shield.

The possible contribution must be a justified difference estimator/operator or counterexample-coverage rule. Alpha conditioning plus reachability plus line search currently looks like an integration of established components. Policy hashes establish provenance; they do not provide gradients. Each label must identify scene/dynamics, integration, goal, remaining horizon, continuation parameters, and history/carry convention. Candidate carry must be recomputed causally on the declared prefix; teacher carry cannot silently substitute. A fixed-old-continuation Q cannot validate changed future actions.

Before training, derive what the estimator bounds and test its counterexamples. Later, matched-budget controls must include direct paired full-policy rollout acceptance, cleaned unique-ID replay, and an ordinary constrained-RL reference. Compare original-start deterministic outcomes, critic ranking/calibration on held-out continuation branches, sample/compute cost, and each retained ID. Smooth learned margins and finite tests provide no global certificate near contact discontinuities. If direct paired acceptance works equally well, or uncertainty rejection prevents all useful learning, the proposed extra mechanism has not earned its complexity.

This audit supports investigating that narrow question. It supplies neither a solved algorithm nor evidence of novelty or improved Stage performance.
