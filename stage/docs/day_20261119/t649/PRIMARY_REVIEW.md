## Objective and correct gradient interpretation

Explicitly introduce the terminal objective `R = 1` for native safe GOAL and `0` otherwise, with no discount or time shaping. Leave stored simulator rewards untouched. This is a newly selected optimization objective, not a correction to the old reward and not an original algorithm. For task `j`, rollout `i`, use `b_ji = sum_{k != i} R_jk / 3` and fixed `A_ji = R_ji - b_ji`.

For `J(theta) = (1/19) sum_j E_theta[R | task j]`, the collection-policy score estimate is

`g = (1/(19*4)) sum_ji A_ji sum_t grad log p_theta(a_jit | full recorded history_jit)`.

The other three independent trajectories give a baseline independent of the scored trajectory; its expected product with that trajectory's total score is zero. This is the standard leave-one-out construction, present in [Kool et al.'s author poster/formula](https://wouterkool.github.io/pdf/poster-buy-4-samples-free-baseline.pdf). Use the **sum** over episode time. Dividing each episode by its action-dependent length changes this objective's weighting. A fixed common factor such as 1/512 only scales the gradient. Do not divide by a reward standard deviation estimated from these same samples when claiming an unbiased score estimate. All-four-equal outcome groups have zero LOO signal; report the number of informative groups. The proposed minimum of two informative new-task groups is a predeclared collection-quality gate, not proof that four samples estimate a task reliably. The unbiased identity is unconditional over iid collection; conditioning training on this outcome-dependent gate does not justify an unbiasedness claim conditional on passing it.

With `r_t = p_theta(a_t|h_t) / p_theta0(a_t|h_t)`, maximize the task/episode-equal **sum** of `min(r_t*A, clip(r_t,.8,1.2)*A)`, divided by the same fixed 512, minus the shared old-response loss. At theta0, before clipping/repeated updates and before optimizer/gradient clipping, the PG component has the score-gradient interpretation above. Subsequent clipped epochs on fixed histories are a **PPO-style surrogate**, not an unbiased gradient of current-policy success probability. The replay term also explicitly changes the optimization objective. [PPO §2.1, §3 Eq.7 and Algorithm1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.06347) motivate this sampling/optimization structure and also warn about destructive repeated plain-PG updates. Call this "critic-free episodic clipped PG with LOO and old replay"; do not call it a full PPO/GAE reproduction. One fresh batch followed by eight epochs is one online collection/update cycle, not eight fresh on-policy rounds.

The more recent [Ahmadian et al. §2.3](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.662.pdf) independently makes the iid assumption explicit for RLOO. Its favorable LLM results do not establish navigation performance, variance reduction for this recurrent task, or old-skill retention.

## Executed-action density and recurrent history

Use the clipped Gaussian's mixed measure. Per action coordinate: interior density is `Normal(mu,.2).pdf(a)`; the -1 atom has probability `Phi((-1-mu)/.2)`; the +1 atom has probability `Phi((mu-1)/.2)`. Sum coordinate log probabilities, then exponentiate the difference of candidate and frozen behavior log probabilities. Preserve raw samples and atom flags for audit. A Gaussian PDF evaluated at an executed boundary is not its atom probability. Scoring the retained unbounded raw sample is a different valid estimator when that raw variable is the sampled action; do not mix the two density conventions. [CAPG §3.1–3.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/fujita18a/fujita18a.pdf) derives the clipped score; the [authors' `ClippedGaussian.log_prob`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pfnet-research/capg/master/clipped_gaussian.py) explicitly selects CDF/survival probabilities at the limits. Adopt these established formulas, not an originality claim.

Reconstruct each candidate's GRU carry from the true episode start using all recorded causal observations and actual previous executed actions, with only the genuine episode-start reset. Recompute hidden states under candidate weights for every update, shadow and independent history reconstruction. Parent hidden states and zero-initialized sampled windows are invalid substitutes after the body changes. Backpropagate through the full reconstructed sequence; document any truncation if later introduced. Old384 trajectories are replay anchors, not asserted current-policy rollouts.
