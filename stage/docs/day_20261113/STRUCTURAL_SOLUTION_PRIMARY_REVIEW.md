# Structural alternatives for Stage consolidation — primary-source review

2026-11-13. Research only: no simulator, optimizer, checkpoint, process, run-directory, or RESEARCH_STATUS mutation. Sources checked on the web; this is a selective nearest-neighbor review, not an exhaustive or novelty-certifying search. Stage only; Phase was not inspected.

## Decision

The strongest *different Stage experiment* is **training-only differentiation through a short closed-loop vehicle-and-observation surrogate, with native directional-derivative validation before Actor training**. Its scientific target is whether trajectory consequences provide usable learning information missing from successful-history action matching. It is not another residual, output projection, hard constraint, or replay-weight adjustment. Keep the deployed Actor exactly causal: 120 current inputs plus its GRU history to drive/steer. The surrogate, physical state, reference trajectory, and any training geometry disappear at deployment.

This is a conditional research recommendation, not a prediction of success or an original general algorithm. Differentiable imitation, closed-loop driving training, and learned differentiable surrogates already have direct precedents. The potentially useful Stage contribution would be a demonstrated, native-validated gradient mechanism under its restrictive sensor/history contract, followed by joint old/new closed-loop improvement. It has not been established.

## What the local evidence does and does not say

The accepted reference remains T507/T508 old8=8/8 and field=11/24. T527 learned autonomous sector0_offset2 GOAL but lost old skills (old8=6/8); its complete field24 was not tested. T531 directly optimized 20 successful causal histories, three families balanced: its final report records all executed audits passed, old8=8/8 and field=11/24, but loss of the new goal and baseline sector2_offset2; the candidate was rejected and rolled back. Its registered plan explicitly identifies fixed-history coverage as unresolved. See [current status](../RESEARCH_STATUS.md), [T527 report](T527_FINAL_REPORT.json), [T531 final report](T531_FINAL_REPORT.json), and [T531 plan](T531_PLAN.md). This review does not promote either research candidate.

These observations establish repeated **closed-loop retention/acquisition tradeoffs** in these finite trials. They do not distinguish insufficient causal observability, a poor representation, loss of plasticity, a difficult parameter basin, conflicting labels, or distribution shift as a unique cause. T511 frozen residual and T516–T518 nonlinear/hard-constraint/tangent methods already failed their registered tests; renaming one of those is not a structural alternative. A prior sparse SAC pilot failing is also not a controlled rejection of all joint recurrent off-policy training.

## The proposed structural change

Current successful-history supervision minimizes disagreement at observations generated by successful source policies. It does not directly differentiate how the student's slightly different actions alter its later observations, GRU state, and trajectory. The proposed graph instead contains:

`Actor(o_t, h_t) -> strict actuator -> vehicle state -> causal observation -> Actor(o_(t+1), h_(t+1)) -> finite-window trajectory loss`.

All nodes after the Actor are **training-only**. The loss can use a stored successful physical trajectory as a training reference, with fixed local pose/heading/speed scales and old/new sequence balancing. These are labels from actual success, not runtime waypoints. For the first diagnostic use a local smooth trajectory loss, not a discontinuous GOAL/contact bit and not an unvalidated learned terminal value. This tests useful local sensitivity, not the long-horizon navigation objective. Eventual acceptance still requires original-start autonomous old8 and complete field24, including retention of every accepted field ID and the new goal.

An ideal kinematic bicycle alone is not an adequate claimed model. The strict squared-drive/direct-steering mapping, actuator response, action hold, physical integration timing, velocity/slip effects, goal coordinates, time features, observation normalization, and sensor/history construction must be accounted for or their omission validated. Forward closed-loop simulation must regenerate observations as the state changes. This is distinct from differentiating every observation path: deliberate stop-gradients or temporal decay are legitimate approximate learning signals if reported and tested. Freezing the stored 120-input sequence in the forward rollout instead tests a different, teacher-forced problem. A ray/obstacle observation model may itself switch surfaces discontinuously. Do not silently use extra state as deployed policy input to make the model work.

The drone author's [training code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HenryHuYu/DiffPhysDrone/master/main_cuda.py) calls `env.render` each timestep and detaches position in its target-velocity construction; [environment code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HenryHuYu/DiffPhysDrone/master/env_cuda.py) renders into an empty canvas through a CUDA call without an explicit renderer autograd wrapper, while dynamics use a custom backward function with gradient decay. Thus forward feedback is present, but the inspected code does not justify claiming all sensor derivatives are propagated. The proposed native finite-difference gate tests usefulness of the chosen Stage backward approximation; it is not a universal requirement satisfied by every cited paper or proof of replicating the drone method.

This changes the source of credit: a change in action is judged by predicted *state consequences under recurrent feedback*. Unlike SAC, the Actor gradient flows through explicit dynamics/observation derivatives rather than solely through a learned action-value gradient. Unlike T517/T518, there is no proposed projection intended to freeze old outputs. It can still fail to find a common policy, overfit the surrogate, or produce a direction that harms old tasks.

## Minimal falsifiable diagnostic before Actor training

This is a proposal for a separately registered bounded protocol, not authorization to run it in this research task or to extend the 17:30 deadline. Zero Actor/Value optimizer steps throughout; keep accepted artifacts immutable.

1. **Contract gate, existing data only.** Reconstruct full causal hidden state from episode start for a fixed small set of already recorded old/new windows. Assert the original Actor action sequence matches existing evidence. Evaluate an analytic, untrained surrogate against recorded transitions/observations. If required physical or observation state is absent, record “insufficient evidence”; do not reconstruct it from future information or assume a finite-difference test has happened. Fit a model only in a separately counted later protocol if analytic accuracy fails.
2. **Freeze the hypothesis before native branches.** Select four windows by prior event rules: old successful traversal, old candidate failure before first contact, newgoal accepted-parent miss, and newgoal T527 success. Keep them short (for example 1 second, with each native decision hold unchanged). Choose windows strictly before existing contact. Fix a smooth reference-state loss and scales. Choose two parameter directions: one deterministic control direction and one normalized negative surrogate gradient. Compute gradients only; take no optimizer step. Perturbation amplitudes must keep actions local, inside unclipped output support.
3. **End-to-end directional test.** For each window and direction, compare the chosen surrogate gradient `g · d` with the native symmetric difference `[L(theta+eps*d)-L(theta-eps*d)]/(2*eps)`, at eps and eps/2. This is 32 short native branches plus any explicitly budgeted exact baseline/restoration checks. Restore complete physical integration state. For each perturbed Actor, recompute its own causal GRU hidden state from the same prefix; perturbing weights while reusing the old hidden state checks a different gradient. Future observations come from each native branch, not the stored reference sequence. With stop-gradients/decay, call `g` an approximate descent signal rather than the exact derivative of the forward surrogate. The comparison tests parameter sensitivity through actual recurrent feedback, beyond just first-action steering sensitivity.
4. **Predeclare a stringent small-test gate.** Example provisional engineering thresholds: baseline prediction errors below 2 cm and 1 degree over the window; finite-difference magnitudes stable within 25% across the two amplitudes; matching sign for every resolved old and new derivative; surrogate relative derivative error at most 25% where the native effect exceeds ten times exact-replay numerical error. These values are suggestions requiring prior protocol registration, not thresholds retrospectively chosen after looking at branches. Near-zero derivatives are unresolved, not passes. Any contact, early termination, sensor-surface switch that spoils scale stability, or old/new-specific sign failure stops Actor learning under this surrogate; do not average it away.

A pass establishes only a small local derivative agreement region. With physical state restored at a window and hidden state reconstructed from a fixed recorded prefix, this is a conditional-window derivative; the surrogate must use the same prefix/hidden gradient convention. It is not the derivative of a newly perturbed episode from its original physical start. It neither verifies an exact native gradient globally nor guarantees a safe finite update or eventual GOAL. A failure is scientifically useful: it rejects the proposed credit mechanism before another consolidation training trial. The next *learning* experiment, only after a pass, would compare one bounded trajectory-gradient proposal with a matched action-BC proposal, use the same initial Actor and old/new data, and measure original-start retention/acquisition rather than just training loss. No extra seed/model/horizon search follows automatically.

## Why the other directions are secondary now

- **Joint recurrent off-policy training from scratch:** a clean alternative to repeatedly editing a mature representation, supported as a baseline by Ni et al. It requires substantial fresh interaction and a trustworthy recurrent Critic; neither replay nor a fresh initialization alone is a mechanism test. It is a later bounded baseline, not a renamed sparse SAC restart or something that can be inferred successful from the literature.
- **Student-occupancy distillation:** targets the right distribution rather than only source histories. However, T529/T530 already show that a source policy need not be a valid recovery teacher on student-induced states. A new aggregation proposal needs an expert competent on those actual causal histories, not more takeover-time sweeping or relabeling failed recovery as success.
- **True action chunks:** predicting future action sequences changes the temporal output model, unlike simply carrying a one-step GRU. It also introduces open-loop exposure and a deployment-contract question; issuing a chunk without checking intermediate feedback is not an established cure for old/new interference. Test temporal error structure before changing the output contract.
- **Plasticity repair or larger memory:** T527's new learning with old degradation is not evidence of an inability to learn at all. Activation/rank or history-aliasing measurements could motivate these changes, but failed BC alone does not. Do not reinitialize the retained Actor or introduce task routing on this diagnosis.

The concrete next knowledge gain is therefore: **can a small, physically faithful, causally closed-loop surrogate predict native trajectory derivatives for both old and new behavior?** The evidence currently does not answer it.
