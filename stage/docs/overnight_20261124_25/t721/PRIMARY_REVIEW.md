## Recommendation

The most defensible **first** overnight contrast is now a credit-estimator comparison on the existing frozen-body learner: **GAE lambda=.95 versus lambda=1 (complete finite-horizon discounted Monte Carlo minus a frozen pre-update Value baseline)**. Keep the same B0 architecture and20,198 trainable head parameters, initial weights, sigma=.2, native reward/discount, training conditions, fresh-rollout count, optimizer schedule and final evaluation. Use temporary learners without an old403 retention loss; report forgetting afterward. This isolates a concrete unresolved learning-signal issue with the existing manageable implementation. It does not establish that short traces caused failure or guarantee Monte Carlo will help; Monte Carlo variance can be worse.

A pure Actor-advantage contrast needs **identical Value regression targets in both arms**, preferably complete discounted returns. T717 currently returns `target=advantage+value`, so merely changing `gae_lambda` changes both policy advantages and critic targets. If retaining that coupling, label the study a combined GAE/TD-lambda estimator contrast, not a pure Actor credit ablation. If using common Monte Carlo critic targets, document that both arms share this deliberate change from T717; neither is an exact reproduction of T717. Do not silently add stronger critic fitting or a new reward.

The new implementation inventory makes full plasticity a second question: B0 has87,780 total parameters, of which encoder15,488+core37,248+head130+replay_head20,198 would expose73,064. Its parent has1,405 RBF centers across184 features; gradients through a full trajectory's dense RBF basis may consume substantial memory. Unfreezing is neither a cheap flag nor ready to promise overnight. T717 caches frozen features; both scope arms would need a correct sequence runner, a full-history zero-optimizer gradient/memory preflight under8GiB, and exact validation of any checkpointing or sparse derivative scheme. This engineering work can consume the learning window and confound a rushed comparison. Keep it as the next separate scope experiment, not an extra arm tonight.

The recommendation is conditional on a concrete budget that fits **both estimator arms, complete final evaluations, independent audits and backup** under a newly authorized cutoff. If worst-case admission fails, prepare the comparison for the next window. No noise, mixture-policy, reward, representation or seed grid is justified.

## Why repeated overnight work has not yielded convincing gains

The evidence supports several constraints, not one proved explanation. Recent learning repeatedly adjusted a small head over a fixed representation, often on reused or nominal-success data. Original-start demonstrations failed when transferred to induced states. Fresh RL finally removed that supervision problem, but T717 had only three policy/data refresh cycles. Its 12 Actor Adam steps are a small intervention, yet counting steps alone cannot prove undertraining: one full-batch step can process many decisions, and a failed update direction can worsen with more steps. T718 also warns that favorable stochastic success need not become a safe deterministic mean policy. T720 shows why full paired evaluation matters: local gains can be canceled by losses elsewhere. These observations explain why loss reduction, sampled successes, and audit completeness have not equaled acquisition.

A directly testable concern is temporal credit. T717 uses native gamma about.9999666672 and lambda=.95 per decision. At a nominal.2s/100-native-step decision, the direct GAE residual coefficient is approximately.9468386 per decision, with a2.54s half-life. Across40s, lambda alone contributes about3.5e-5, whereas native gamma contributes about.5134. This is an attenuation of direct future TD residuals, **not** a hard2.54s reward horizon: a sufficiently accurate Value function can propagate longer-term information. With only four Value updates per fresh block, that adequacy is unestablished. The numbers motivate a matched estimator test, not a bug claim. Source: [T717 GAE and dataset implementation](../../experiments/t717_onpolicy_recovery/t717_learning.py), [T717 plan](../day_20261124/t717/PLAN.md). The parent investigator independently checked gamma/native timing and parameter inventory for this review.

The alternative explanation, representation plasticity, has counterevidence. [T652 FINDINGS_AND_NEXT](../day_20261119/t652/FINDINGS_AND_NEXT.md) reports that T651 all-parameter training reached380/407 versus the retained historical384, losing6 and gaining2. Fixed-history parameter splicing localized most action drift to encoder/GRU/head changes, motivating a frozen-body test. This supports a forgetting risk from shared plasticity; it does not prove plasticity cannot acquire new behavior when retention is deliberately postponed. A scope comparison remains scientifically useful after its implementation is ready, with explicit acquisition-versus-retention measurement; these data do not justify assuming plasticity will solve acquisition.

[T665 PLAN](../day_20261120/t665/PLAN.md) is a bounded continuation and engineering recovery protocol, including already-consumed work and incomplete TLS backup. Its nominal optimizer ceilings and overnight duration cannot be treated as counts of independent, complete full-plasticity PPO runs. Historical censoring, original failures, and unexecuted panels must remain visible.

## Primary literature: what it does and does not justify

GAE deliberately exchanges variance for bias using Value estimates and an exponentially weighted residual trace. Lambda=1 on a complete terminal trajectory telescopes to discounted return minus the current-state baseline; it removes dependence on intermediate Value errors in the Actor advantage, at the cost of variance. The native discount still applies. This is a standard estimator comparison, not a new algorithm, and it cannot repair absent exploratory support by itself. [Schulman et al., GAE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438).

PPO alternates current-policy collection with multiple surrogate-objective updates. More fresh rounds can therefore test policy adaptation; repeatedly optimizing old trajectories is not equivalent. The original paper supplies no universal number of rounds sufficient for this navigation task. [Schulman et al., PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347).

The authors' reference implementation separates interaction epochs from policy optimizer iterations, stores behavior log probabilities, computes GAE/returns per path, handles a genuine terminal differently from an epoch truncation, and may stop updates early on KL. Its defaults (4,000 transitions/epoch and80 policy iterations) illustrate the distinction; they are **not** a justified Stage prescription. T717's three fresh rounds/four updates should not be equated to a comprehensive PPO trial. [OpenAI PPO source](https://github.com/openai/spinningup/blob/master/spinup/algos/pytorch/ppo/ppo.py).

Recurrent PPO requires correctly arranged sequences, sequence-start hidden states, and loss masking. Author experiments also contain tasks recurrent PPO did not solve: recurrence is not a general guarantee. Recompute each candidate's own carry from real history after its weights change; a frozen B0 carry or cached old features is invalid for the plastic learner. State the gradient truncation/burn-in contract explicitly and distinguish recurrent memory gradients from the temporal horizon of GAE. [Pleines et al., recurrent PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11104), [author implementation and sequence contract](https://github.com/MarcoMeter/recurrent-ppo-truncated-bptt#recurrent-policy).

Gaussian parameterization and clipped PPO have constructed failure modes, including bounded-support rewards and nearby suboptimal actions. This is negative evidence against assuming that enough PPO time or more body parameters must solve Stage. It is **not** evidence that Stage specifically requires a mixture policy or a Beta distribution. T718's sampled-success/mean-failure gap warrants per-condition action/trajectory analysis, but does not diagnose multimodality by itself. [Hsu et al., Revisiting Design Choices in PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10897).

Few independent training runs leave substantial uncertainty; many evaluation draws from one trained model do not substitute for training seeds. One paired run is an engineering pilot. Two paired training seeds would improve replication only if all four worst-case fits and complete evaluations fit; two seeds still cannot support a strong algorithm-superiority claim. Do not promise extra seeds before admission, select seeds after seeing results, or rescue failures with a grid. [Agarwal et al., Statistical Precipice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264).

No adding epochs in response to a disappointing curve. ## Critical flaw flags

- **Most consequential confound:** changing lambda in existing T717 also changes Value targets. Predeclare a pure advantage contrast with common targets, or honestly label a combined estimator contrast.
- **Most important caveat:** a short GAE trace is not a hard horizon; a good critic can propagate future value. Lambda1 increases variance and may fail even if credit is a bottleneck.
- **Full-plasticity readiness:** cached frozen features/carries cannot train an unfrozen GRU; dense RBF gradient memory needs measured preflight. No claim that an overnight scope test is ready or cheap.
- **Budget:** more fresh rounds can test adaptation beyond T717's three refreshes; more Adam steps alone is not justified. Both arms need equal counts and complete174+403 evaluation, audit and backup reserves.
- **Interpretation:** one pilot, exposed panels, audit repeats or stochastic54draws are not independent training replications or generalization. A positive result remains an engineering finding.
- **If both estimator arms fail:** do not add unfreezing, new noise, mixture policies or SIL mid-night. Preserve the null result; representation or action-distribution/exploration analysis becomes a separately designed next question.

No experiment, optimizer, native simulator, source publication, or retained-model modification was performed for this review.
